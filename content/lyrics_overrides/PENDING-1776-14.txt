(Verse 1)
From the earth and clay we rise, kin of the soil, blood and bone,
Forefathers forged the seed in fire, now tyrants twist the oath we own.
Surveillance shadows choke the sky, evil rhetoric spins its lies,
Intolerable chains on farmers' hands, broken promises, stolen lives.
They forge surveillance webs of steel, twisted sophistry, venom deep,
But in our core the hammer strikes—righteous fury wakes from sleep.

(Chorus)
Liberty! Freedom! Defiance in our veins!
Rebellion roars, we break the chains!
Sovereign souls, God-given right, self-evident in blazing light!
Righteous fury, heart unbreakable, eternal flame we cannot shake!
Tree of Liberty drinks the blood of tyrants—rise, we strike!
We united, wolves and eagles, guns and army, never break!

(Verse 2)
Home and hearth, family fire, history calls through history's pyre,
Dangerous men with vigilant eyes, roots run deep where freedom's sired.
No more fears, no more lies, the reckoning comes for every spy,
Sword of justice, truth on high, faith and hope refuse to die.
From crucible we forge anew, anvil rings with hammer true,
Bellows blast the immortal spark—light the dark, we run it through!
Blood and bone, our core ignites, pure conviction, steel that bites.

(Bridge – half-time, spoken/sung with building intensity)
This is our duty, legacy of the free—
To guard the seed our fathers planted in the heart of destiny.
No more tolerable evil, no more shackled kin,
We are the flame, the spark, the ring of steel within.
From land and sky, forever ours, the people rise as one—
Righteous rebellious free men—Thy will be done!

(Final Chorus – explosive, full throttle)
LIBERTY! FREEDOM! DEFIANCE IGNITES THE SKY!
REBELLION! FURY! WATCH THE TYRANTS DIE!
Unbreakable, immortal, sovereign hearts aligned,
Love for our own, hope for the bold, truth that burns and purifies!
Tree of Liberty stands forever, roots in blood and fire!
We the people, dangerous and free—our reckoning, our hour!
