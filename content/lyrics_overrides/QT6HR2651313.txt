She came from broken soil where dreams lay torn and frayed,
Carried burdens heavy as the debts that went unpaid.
Watched the ones she loved dissolve like mist at dawn,
Learned to stand when all the pillars were withdrawn.
With trembling hands she built a life from shattered stone,
Turned quiet tears to rivers carving paths of her own.
The world it tried to bend her, twist her, make her small,
But in the fire she discovered she could hold it all.

Her soul’s been tested by the cruelest hands of fate,
And still she offers mercy, never sealing off her gate.
Valiant in her silence, thunder in her veins,
In broken places beauty and resilience remains.
Love that stands unyielding when the whole world shakes,
Always pouring out the grace that sorrow takes.
Havilah, Havilah — a living, breathing prayer.

Years of silent battles no one saw her wage alone,
Loss that carved deep rivers in the marrow of her bone.
She raised the young through hunger, fear, and pouring rain,
Gave them roots and wings while weathering her pain.
Betrayals cut like knives, yet forgiveness found its way,
Hard-won wisdom blooming where the hurt once held its sway.
Now her voice carries comfort soft as morning dew,
Strength wrapped gentle in the light she offers you.

She doesn’t claim the victory without the cost,
Knows that every battle leaves its mark upon the lost.
Yet she chooses kindness like a sword of purest light,
Turns her pain to purpose in the stillness of the night.
Grace upon grace, she moves like rivers to the sea,
Showing us that broken things can set the captives free.
