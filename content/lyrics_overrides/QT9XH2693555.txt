(Verse 1)
In the quiet hours before the dawn would break,
You stood tall through the storms no one else could take.
Seven hearts you carried, seven flames you raised,
With hands that never trembled, through every weary phase.
Strong and enduring, like iron in the fire,
Your will was the hammer, your love the pure desire.

(Chorus)
Oh Mother, my blacksmith, in the crucible of home,
You pounded out the weakness, you shaped us as your own.
Battle-tested in your forge, where the flames refined,
We rose as children of God, with an unbreakable bind.
My metal now rings of you, clear and true it sings,
I shine with all your light, the strength your heart still brings.

(Verse 2)
You were the fire that burned the doubt away,
The steady hand that guided us through the fray.
Pounding out the darkness, tempering every soul,
In the heat of family, you made broken pieces whole.
Resilient warrior, with a spirit forged in grace,
You gave us roots unyielding, in this sacred place.

(Chorus)
Oh Mother, my blacksmith, in the crucible of home,
You pounded out the weakness, you shaped us as your own.
Battle-tested in your forge, where the flames refined,
We rose as children of God, with an unbreakable bind.

(Bridge)
Every scar I carry is a medal from your hand,
Every step I take echoes where you stand.
You didn’t just raise children—you forged a legacy,
An armor of faith and love that lives eternally.
Through trials and through triumph, your light never fades,
In me and in my siblings, your beautiful song plays.

(Final Chorus – softer, then building)
Oh Mother, my amazing, strong and enduring one,
You are the forge that made us, beneath the rising sun.
We shine because you loved us, with a will so pure and bright,
Unbreakable forever, in your everlasting light.
My metal rings of you... I shine with all your light.
