We forge the stars from iron will, from secrets buried deep.
Hammers ring on anvil hearts, sparks fly like sacred cries,
Men and heavens born in fire, under endless, watchful skies.
From bone and flesh, the breath of life ignites the core,
Sacrifice the bellows blow, grit and blood forevermore.
No easy dawn, no gentle spark—only the burning desire,
To shape the soul in molten heat, lift valor ever higher.
Through the forge we rise, unbreakable,
Faith and courage, conviction pure,
Determination's thunder roars,
Valor stands, forever sure.
