They told you the hearth was a cage and a chain,
That your gentle hands held no real power to gain.
They whispered the lie that a mother’s soft light
Was weakness disguised in the still of the night.
But the truth cuts through like a sword from above—
You were never less; you were fashioned in love.
The womb that brings life when the world says “decline,”
Is the fiercest rebellion this dark age will find.
No rhetoric, no chains of their clever deceit,
Can steal what the Father has placed at your feet.
You carry the spark that turns houses to homes,
The courage that stands when the whole world roams.
Rise up, sacred steel, in your beautiful might!
Giver of life, in the hush of the night.
With arms like a fortress, with love like a flame,
You bind every heart in the family’s name.
Hope in your eyes, courage forged in your prayer,
Power that tyrants could never compare.
Selfless and holy, you pour out your all—
Under God you stand tall… you will never fall.
They called it oppression to nurture and tend,
To wipe away tears and to teach them again.
But every lullaby, every meal on the plate,
Is a battlefield win that decides a child’s fate.
No king without his queen—his true strength flows from you;
Without that fierce love, what would any man do?
You shape kings and kingdoms through family’s grace,
Elevating the world from your quiet, holy place.
They sold you the lie that your strength must be loud,
But the loudest of victories never need crowds.
In the midnight watch when the babies all cry,
Your steel never bends—there’s a King on high.
Rise up, sacred steel, in your beautiful might!
Giver of life, in the hush of the night.
With arms like a fortress, with love like a flame,
You bind every heart in the family’s name.
Hope in your eyes, courage forged in your prayer,
Power that tyrants could never compare.
Selfless and holy, you pour out your all—
Under God you stand tall… you will never fall.
One life laid down in the most sacred way—
Nine months of fire just to give them the day.
Years of your youth poured like wine on the floor,
So your children will rise and remember the Lord.
No medal, no crown that the world could bestow
Can match what you’ve given… the seeds that you sow.
When the night feels too long and the voices grow loud,
Hear the whisper of heaven: “My daughter, stand proud.”
Rise up, sacred steel, let the whole world see!
Woman of wonder, forever set free.
Not chained by their lies, but enthroned in His plan—
The heart of the home is where kingdoms began.
Hope, love, and courage—your banner unfurled,
Power that changes the face of the world.
Selfless and mighty, with faith like a shield,
You are everything beautiful… God’s sacred steel.
To every woman who gives till she’s poured out and spent…
Heaven is watching… and your crown is already bent.
You are hope. You are love. You are courage. You are power.
The light that holds families through every dark hour.
Darkness is nothing… just absence of you…
Rise up, sacred steel… the world needs you.
