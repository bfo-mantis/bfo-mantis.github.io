Brothers, we stand rooted deep in this red clay and stone,
Feet planted firm on the American soil we call home.
Our Bloodline is Unity, our values have made us one kin,
Every scar from the fight, every loss that we’ve taken within.
We laid our fathers down in the earth that still echoes their cry,
Now the ground beneath us demands we no longer comply.

Rain falls heavy on this hard and sacred land,
The weight of lost freedom presses down… but here we make our stand.

Brotherhood — it’s the unbreakable vow when tyrants try to chain our heart,
We brothers forged stronger through the fire that tears us apart.
We hold each other equal in the dust where true men are tried,
We guard the underdog fiercely till oppression’s grip has died.
With liberty’s flame burning wild and deep in our soul,
We rise as one under God — defiant, sovereign, and whole.
Untamed through the pain, brave and ever vigilant,
This is brotherhood — now brothers, rise and take it back again.

When the floods of control crash hard and the shadows close in tight,
When the odds stack cruel against every God-given right,
We dig our heels deeper in the mud that remembers our name,
Drawing iron strength from the land that refuses to tame.
Side by side we unite to shatter every chain that would bind,
We rebel as one against the forces that steal what is mine and thine.
No king, no system, no darkness this age tries to send
Can break what our shared blood and trials have welded in the end.

When the whole world crumbles and the voices scream “surrender now,”
We lock shoulders in the dirt, feel the earth rise up and avow —
The burden may weigh heavy, the road may cut us to the bone,
But you’re not heavy… you’re my brother — together we carry it home.
Come what may, through the fire, through the loss that cuts us deep,
We choose never to walk alone — this vow we fiercely keep.
Loyalty born in adversity, tested where free men are made,
Now brothers, the call is clear: rally for the rebellion — liberty must not fade.
