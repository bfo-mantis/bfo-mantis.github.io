In the silence where a seeking soul collides with holy fire,
I bow to no man’s fleeting throne, no tyrant’s cold empire.
Not to Caesar’s rusted scepter or the howling mob’s desire—
But the Sovereign who breathed galaxies and birthed the dawn’s first pyre.

What broken chains dare bind the free whom the Eternal has released?
What arrogant decree can steal what the Divine has already decreed?

What did God endow when He shaped us from the dust and clay?
Life and Liberty, the pursuit of Happiness—unalienable blaze!
Rights no government can grant, no parliament can take away,
Endowed by our Creator—here true sovereignty holds its sway!
Where does liberty come from? Not from thrones or ballot kings,
But from the Father’s thunderous breath that makes the spirit scream and sing.
Sovereign souls, ignite! Let freedom’s holy wildfire burn and sting
Every lie that sells us velvet chains dressed up as offering.

They poison tender minds with godless creeds and velvet lies,
Claiming rights are privileges rulers generously disguise.
But seventeen seventy-six still thunders through the skies:
“Endowed by their Creator”—watch that truth make tyrants bleed and die.
Natural law carved in every heart by prophets, blood, and sages,
Image-bearers stamped with glory, dignity that rages
Against the State that steals what Heaven’s own hand engages.

O Lord, forgive how far we drifted into softer, subtler cages,
Awake us from this comfortable coma, these soul-killing stages.
Slice straight through the marrow, purge our fear and compromises,
Rekindle sovereign fire that no darkness ever extinguishes.

No more groveling serfs beneath Leviathan’s iron glare,
No trading birthrights for the poison pottage of false welfare.
Hearts ablaze, we shatter every shackle, every snare—
Liberty’s fountain flows from You alone, beyond compare.

What did God endow? Rights eternal, fierce, and true!
Liberty’s ’s sacred origin livz and dies alone in You!
Sovereign under Heaven—let the battle anthem break right through,
Ignite our hearts forever—we were born to fight for You!
