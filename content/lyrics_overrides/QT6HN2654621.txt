One voice.
One command.
One fire that will not kneel.

Winter fields of Domrémy still freeze beneath the boot,
yet the air splits open—Michael, Catherine, Margaret—
iron tongues of heaven cutting through the fog.
Seventeen years of earth and hunger answer without pause.
I take the road the captains fear to name,
banner white against the blackened sky,
no bloodline, no commission, only the weight of the unseen.
Orléans starves behind its walls; I ride straight into the teeth.
Nine days of iron and prayer, the English line collapses,
the river runs with their retreat.
A peasant girl in men’s plate, sword found beneath the altar stone,
I do not ask the generals’ leave—I give the order and the day obeys.

Every doubt is ash beneath the hoof.
Every chain is measured against the vow.

Devotion!
Steel and sacrament, the only law I know.
Devotion!
When the banners fall I raise the only standard left.
Devotion!
Through the breach, through the smoke, through the judgment hall—
Devotion!
I answer still.

Reims opens its gates like a wound that finally bleeds clean.
Crown the rightful king while the enemy watches from the dark.
I ride at the front where the crossbows speak,
hair cut short, voice cutting louder than the captains’ horns.
Then the trap closes at Compiègne—Burgundian hands, English gold.
They strip the armor, bind the wrists, drag the girl who led an army
into a room of learned men who smell of ink and fear.
They circle with questions sharpened for the noose:
“Who spoke? By what right? In what form?”
I give them only the truth that burns their parchment:
the voices remain. The command remains.
I will not renounce the light that set the kingdom free.

Every threat is measured against the vow.
Every flame is only another road.

Devotion!
Steel and sacrament, the only law I know.
Devotion!
When the banners fall I raise the only standard left.
Devotion!
Through the breach, through the smoke, through the judgment hall—
Devotion!
I answer still.

They offer the paper of recantation.
I take the pen, then cast it down.
Male attire again—defiance stitched into the cloth.
The court erupts. The sentence falls like a blade.
Rouen’s marketplace. The stake is raised.
They bind me higher than the common dead
so every eye can watch the heretic burn.
Smoke climbs. The voices do not leave.
I call the name that never failed me
while the fire finds its work.

Devotion.
In the marrow.
Devotion.
In the bone.
Devotion.
When the body fails the vow remains.
Devotion.
I answer still.

Devotion!
Steel and sacrament, the only law I know.
Devotion!
When the banners fall I raise the only standard left.
Devotion!
Through the breach, through the smoke, through the judgment hall,
through the pyre that cannot unmake the word—
Devotion!
I answer still.

One song.
One fire.
Devotion.
