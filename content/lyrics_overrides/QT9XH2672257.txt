The Battle of Athens.
Tennessee.
McMinn County. August. Forty-six.
They had the jail. We had the war still in our hands.
Fee per arrest. Fee per night in a cage.
God gave the will. We didn't ask twice.

They stole it in thirty-six with a smile and a lie
Crump in Memphis, Cantrell local, the fee system high
A dollar a body, a night in the cage
Law as a business, the badge as a wage
Three thousand sons of this dirt went to learn how to kill
Came home to a sheriff who billed them at will
The book disappeared when a veteran signed
Freedom got folded and stuffed in a fine

We wrote to the Bureau. We wrote to the throne.
Paper don't bleed and a letter ain't bone.
They don't go good. They go fat on the land.
Power is a hog that eats from your hand.

Counted as cast — or counted in fire
God gave us will, not a hymn for the liar
Life and the liberty no badge can claim
We don't beg a thief to remember our name
Good men don't wait for a kinder machine
They stand in the street with the might they mean
One man can turn. One fuse. One spine.
Bring out the boxes or we blow it down tonight

Shot for insisting a free man can act
The round went through him. He lived. Hear it straight.
The night went black anyway. That's how it breaks.
Watchers in cuffs. The boxes in cells.

White was a Marine with the Pacific still wet
Seventeen at the harbor, not finished yet
He took the poor boys, the ones who could bust a cap
Fighting Bunch — thirty spines in a row, no map
Armory doors. Enfields in the bed of a truck
Thompsons and bandoliers. That was enough.
Nine o'clock, White Street, he stepped in the lamp
"Bring those damn boxes out or we blow this jail to sand"
He sent the first round. Brick learned a new psalm
Six hours of thunder teaching thieves how to calm

Ambulance in the small hours — cowards in white
Cantrell ran. Mansfield ran. The deputies kept the night
Molotovs kissed brick and died like a wish
Then dynamite crawled on the porch like a fist
One man. One charge. One door off the frame
Two-thousand-one-seventy-five wrote the name
Knox Henry. The hog on its back in the street
No one died. That's the miracle. That's the receipt.

They called it a riot.
We called it the count.
The Times said a sheriff was slain.
He wasn't. He ran.
Words without might are a hymn for the owned.
God gave the will to defend what He named.
White used it.
That is the whole of the law that remains.
Stay awake. Stay DANGEROUS.
