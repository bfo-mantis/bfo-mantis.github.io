Before any king or parliament ever drew a breath,
The Creator spoke the truth that echoes beyond death.
Every soul born of dust was stamped with His design—
Life, liberty, and the right to rise and shine.
Not granted by rulers, not loaned by the state,
But woven in our being on the very first day.
These truths stand self-evident, plain as the morning sun—
Written on the heart before the ink was ever run.

Sovereignty means this: the people wear the crown,
Supreme authority flows from Heaven straight down.
We the governed give consent, we alone delegate—
A government of servants, never masters of our fate.
When tyrants twist the law and claim the higher seat,
They forget the iron fact: their power is incomplete.
It was borrowed from the people, who borrowed it from God—
Any chain they forge is counterfeit, a fraud.

Nothing—no throne, no act, no iron boot or lie—
Can stand above the power that comes from on high!
Endowed by the Creator, unalienable and true,
We the people hold the flame, and the flame runs through you.
Self-evident as sunrise, as certain as the dawn,
This is not opinion—this truth was etched in stone!
No higher power exists, no king can override,
The sovereign heart of man stands only at God’s side!

They told us rights were privileges rulers could revoke,
But history wrote the answer in Lexington’s gunsmoke.
When the Intolerable yoke pressed down with all its weight,
The people rose as sovereigns and rewrote their fate.
Because the source is higher than any human hand,
No parliament, no mandate, no global command
Can ever claim dominion where the Creator has spoken—
The power of the people is forever unbroken.

Listen—feel it in your blood, this is not theory or guess,
It’s the law of nature, the law of righteousness.
Governments exist by our leave, not the other way around—
When they forget their place, the sovereign heart makes a sound.
Today the same old chains wear new suits and new names,
But the truth still thunders and the old fire still flames!

Nothing—no throne, no act, no iron boot or lie—
Can stand above the power that comes from on high!
Endowed by the Creator, unalienable and true,
We the people hold the flame, and the flame runs through you.
Self-evident forever, as certain as the dawn,
This is not opinion—this truth was etched in stone!
No higher power exists, no force can override,
Rise up, sovereign children—God is on our side!

The power is yours… because the power is His…
Sovereign by birthright… and it always will be.
This is true.
This is true.
Rise.
