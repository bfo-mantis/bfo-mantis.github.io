(Verse 1: Dawn of Discovery)
In fourteen ninety-two, Columbus sailed the blue,
With sails unfurled 'gainst tempests wild and free.
Not first to touch these shores, but opened freedom's doors,
Where tyrants' chains would break across the sea.
From Jamestown's hardy souls to Plymouth's faithful toll,
They forged a land where liberty could breathe.
Through trials of the wild, the native strife and child,
A new world rose, defying kings' decree.

(Verse 3: Trials and Triumphs)
The Bill of Rights in '91, ten shields 'gainst the gun
Of despot's whim—speech, arms, and faith secure.
