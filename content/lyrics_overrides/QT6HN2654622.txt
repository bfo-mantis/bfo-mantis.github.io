From the Hot Gates the stone still speaks.
Blood of the free runs in the mountain’s veins.
No golden throne shall claim these peaks.
No satrap’s chain shall bind these plains.
The heart that pounds is Leonidas’ own—
unbroken, unbowed, forever alone
in its sovereignty, savage and pure,
defiant to the end the free endure.
Stone remembers. Blood remembers.
The free do not forget.

Hear the thunder of ten thousand feet.
The Immortals march in their endless beat.
Xerxes sits high on his ivory seat,
dreaming of worlds that will kneel at his feet.
Banners of purple, of gold, of the sun
claim the earth as the Great King’s one.
They crossed the Hellespont on bridges of pride.
But the pass is a throat that refuses to swallow.
The mountain is a judge the empire must follow.
Persian overtones rise in the air—
incense, oil, and courtly prayer—
now heaped upon the pyre of our spite,
fuel for the flame that burns through the night.
The sovereign savage answers the fire he has faced.

No knee shall bend in the shadow of kings.
No spirit shall break under empire’s wings.
The heart of Leonidas pounds in our chest—
savage and sovereign, refusing all rest.

Defiant to the savage end!
We stand where the free men defend!
Heart of Leonidas—fire and stone—
sovereign will that will never atone
for the crime of remaining unbound,
for the glory of not going down.
Heap their banners, their gold, their decree
upon the pyre of the forever free!
Raise the cry till the mountains reply—
Defiant! Savage! Sovereign!
We die if we must, but we die on our feet,
unbroken, unyielding, complete.

They offer the velvet of measured chains,
a place among the counted remains.
The soft death of kneeling, the peace of the slave
who forgets the free do not dig their own grave.
But the narrow way knows no compromise.
Blood in the dust still remembers the skies
where eagles of freedom still circle and cry
over men who chose how they would die.
Arrows like locusts darken the day.
Spears of the east stand in forest array.
Yet the phalanx of will does not shatter or yield;
it becomes the anvil on which empires are killed.
Persian silk burns with a beautiful light.
Their pride feeds the flames through the savage night.
The free leave no numbers—only the keys
to a door no tyrant can ever unlock.

Defiant to the savage end!
We stand where the free men defend!
Heart of Leonidas—iron and flame—
sovereign fire that no empire can tame!
Heap their Immortals, their pride, their host
upon the pyre where the free make their boast!
Raise the cry till the stars themselves hear—
Defiant! Savage! Sovereign!

Let the sea of spears arrive!
We answer with the roar of the free!
Let the Immortals drink their fill of the sky!
We answer with what men can be!
No mercy asked from the golden throne.
No quarter given, no mercy shown.
The oldest law of mountain and blood:
the free man decides how he is stood.
He stands. He stands. He stands to the day
when the last of his breath is spent on the cry
that outlives the flesh and refuses to die.

In the dust of the Gates the stories are written
in iron and ash and the blood that is smitten
from bodies that chose not to run or to kneel,
but to forge from their ending a truth that is real.
The heart beats on in the ones who remain.
Leonidas lives in the will and the pain
of every defiant, every sovereign soul
that chooses the fire over empire’s control.
Persian overtones heap on the pyre.
We are not the many. We are the few
who know what it costs to remain true.

Stone remembers the free!
Blood remembers the name!
No throne walks these mountains!
No chain crosses this flame!
We are the spark that sets empires alight!
Defiant to the savage end through the night!
Hearts of iron, of ash, of the unbroken line!
Sovereign fire that will never decline!
Raise it higher! Heap it higher!
Persian gold in the funeral fire!

Defiant to the savage end!
We stand where the free men defend!
Heart of Leonidas—burning and true—
sovereign will that the ages renew!
Heap their glory, their host, their decree
upon the pyre of the forever free!
Raise the cry where the mountains still stand—
Defiant! Savage! Sovereign!
We stand. We stand. We stand. We stand.
Until the last defiant breath is spent—
and even then, the fire remains.

Stone remembers.
Blood remembers.
The free do not forget.
The heart of Leonidas beats still
in every savage sovereign will
that chooses the pyre over the chain,
defiant to the savage end… again.
