In the hush before dawn, on bended knee I fall,
O Sovereign Lord who breathed life into these walls.
You carved liberty in the marrow of my bone,
A sacred fire no mortal king can own.
From the blood-soaked soil where my four fathers stood,
They wove self-evident truths in parchment and wood—
Life, liberty, pursuit—no chain, no crown,
Bill of Rights as armor when the darkness bears down.

Yet shadows creep where eagles once soared high,
Eroding the covenant under veiled lies.
Tyrants in silk whisper, divide, and disarm,
Turning free men to serfs on the altar of harm.
The ink of the founders fades under reckless decree,
But the fire in my chest still burns wild and free.
I see the storm gathering, the chains being forged anew—
Lord, sharpen my spirit, make my courage true.

Give me righteous resolve, steel in my veins,
To stand unyielding through tempests and pain.
Come what may—to the bitter end I fight,
For the God-given birthright, for liberty’s light!
Though the night closes in and the mocking grows loud,
My faith is the anchor, my hope unbowed.
I’ll bleed if I must, I’ll sing as I fall,
But tyranny’s grip will never claim us all!

Hear the cry of the remnant, the prayer of the few,
Who remember the wisdom that liberty grew.
Not in comfort or ease, but in vigilant hearts,
Defending the flame when the whole world departs.
From the hills to the harbors, from mountains to plains,
Let justice awaken in thunderous refrains.
I lift up my country, scarred but still sovereign-born,
Restore what was stolen—let Your truth be reborn.

No retreat. No surrender. No compromise with chains.
Though the cost be my last breath, my legacy remains—
A torch passed forward through fire and flood,
Rooted in Heaven, baptized in blood.
Unbreakable. Unshakable. This I decree—
My soul to my Maker, my sword to the free.

Give me righteous resolve, fire from above,
To fight for the birthright, the country I love.
Come what may—to the bitter end I fight,
With unbreakable faith in the undying light!
Though rights erode and the tyrants advance,
My hope is a banner that will not fall hence.
I pray and I stand—let the heavens reply,
Sovereign liberty… never shall die.

Amen… let it be. Forge us anew, Lord… for liberty.
