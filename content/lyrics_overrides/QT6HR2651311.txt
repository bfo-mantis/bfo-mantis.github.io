In the marrow of midnight, where fractured dreams congeal,
I shape my silhouette from the wreckage of the real.
Storms still whisper treason, gravity draws the veil,
Yet grace rewrites the silence and redraws the cosmic tale.
Echoes of surrender press against these walls,
But the Potter’s quiet fire turns the leaden night to gold.

Every fracture charts the path of my ascent,
Scars become the stars of the unrepentant.

Come what may, through the crucible I blaze,
Fire You first kindled in the hurricane’s gaze.
Shatter me to stardust, scatter my name to the void—
You gather every fragment, Your purpose overjoyed.
No chain can hold the life You placed inside these veins.
Come what may, I rise by Your grace through joy and pain.

But the pull of Your intention lifts me to transcend.
