One man. One bridge. One unbreakable will.
The English army thundered like the sea,
but the berserker stood.
This is the song of what one determined heart can do.
This is the legend that never dies.
From the longships came the thunder, Stamford Bridge ran red,
Harold’s host poured forward like a river of the dead.
Axes high and shields locked tight, the Vikings caught off-guard,
but one lone giant stepped forward, planted boots like iron hard.
No fear in his eyes, just Odin’s fire in his veins,
berserker blood awoke the beast, broke every chain.
He swung that axe in wide arcs, steel sang through flesh and bone,
bodies piled like winter wood, the bridge became his throne.
One man can hold the bridge when the world comes crashing down!
One man with fire in his soul can turn the tide around!
Relentless courage, unyielding stand, unstoppable conviction’s roar—
what one warrior dares to dream, a thousand can do once more!
He held the line, he broke the wave, he laughed at death’s own face—
immortal hero, Viking heart, your spirit lights the age!
They charged in waves of screaming steel, arrows blacked the sky,
he roared and split their helmets, sent their souls to Valhalla high.
Spear and sword could never pierce the rage that burned inside,
every blow he answered harder, every foe he made them die.
The English begged and cursed him, tried to force him from the span,
but he stood like Yggdrasil’s root, rooted in the ancient land.
Hours bled into legend, time itself began to bend—
one man’s will against an army, and the army could not end.
Then the spear came from below, through the planks like serpent’s bite,
but even as he fell he smiled, knowing he had won the fight.
His brothers formed their shield-wall, the battle turned that day,
because one berserker never broke—his stand became the way.
No surrender, no retreat, no compromise in sight—
that is the power of a soul that chooses never to yield the light.
One man can hold the bridge when the world comes crashing down!
One man with fire in his soul can turn the tide around!
Relentless courage, unyielding stand, unstoppable conviction’s roar—
what one warrior dares to dream, the whole world fights for!
He held the line, he broke the wave, he laughed at death’s own face—
immortal hero, Viking heart, your legend claims the age!
From the mountains to the sea, through fire, flood, and endless night—
rise like the berserker, stand and fight!
They carved his name in sagas, in the blood he left behind.
One man. One bridge. One eternal fire.
When the odds are stacked against you, when the darkness calls your name—
remember the berserker.
Stand tall.
Never break.
You are the bridge.
You are the legend.
You are immortal.
