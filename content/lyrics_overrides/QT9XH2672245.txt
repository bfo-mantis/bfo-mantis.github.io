God breathed free will into dust and bone,
A gift no empire, no chain, no throne
Can ever revoke or claim as its own.
The world may rage, may shatter the shell,
But the will inside stands unbreakable—hell
Itself bows low when one man says, “No.”

They may torture my body, break my bones to dust,
Even kill me—then take this husk.
But not my obedience. Not my soul's command.
My will is mine, forged by the Creator's hand.

I am Dangerous—when you push too far.
One free man against your iron star.
Nothing holds higher than the will God gave;
You devour worlds, but I will not be your slave.
I stand immortal in the face of your fire—
Liberty's roots grow thirsty in this hour.
Do not tread on me or mine—
I am your destroyer when the line is crossed.
Peaceful? Yes. Harmless? Never.
Virtue courses through veins that refuse to sever.

The harmless hide in their quiet peace,
No claws, no fire—no virtue's release.
True peace is chosen by the dangerous heart,
The one that could crush you, yet stands apart.
Gandhi faced the lash, the jail, the gun,
Said, “Take my life—but obedience? None.”
One man's refusal turned empires to sand—
That's the power no army can withstand.

Even as you swallow nations whole,
I plant my feet on this sacred soul.
You cannot buy it, break it, or bend—
Free will is the line that never ends.
Self-evident, eternal, carved in divine light:
No power on earth outranks the sovereign fight.
Push me further—watch the storm ignite.

I am Dangerous—because I am free!
God-made will no tyranny can see.
You may break the body, claim the grave—
But my spirit rises, untamed, unchained, brave.
One man holds back your roaring tide,
Becomes legend when he refuses to hide.
Liberty thirsts—its roots drink deep today.
Do not tread on me… or pay the price you’ll pay.
I am peaceful… coursing with virtue’s flame.
Far from harmless—I am the name
You fear in the dark: Dangerous.
Dangerous.
Dangerous.

My will is my own.
You will find me Dangerous…
When pushed.
Do not tread.
The tree drinks deep tonight.
