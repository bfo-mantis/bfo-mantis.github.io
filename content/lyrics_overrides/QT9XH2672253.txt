They pin a badge, they swear an oath, they strut in borrowed might,
Expecting bows and salutes from dawn until the dead of night.
But stripes and stars and black robes don’t make a man divine—
Plenty wear the uniform and still cross every line.
Bad cops plant the evidence, bad judges twist the scale,
Bad soldiers pull the trigger on the innocent and frail.
Politicians sell their souls for lobby cash and power plays,
And “public servants” serve themselves while the people pay and pay.
Blind respect is for sheep, not for those who claim to lead.
You don’t get a free pass just because you wear the creed.
The oath you took was iron—uphold the law, defend the free—
Not follow orders blindly when the orders turn to treachery.
Respect is earned, not owed, you spineless, oath-breaking fraud!
You swore to God and country, not to kiss the tyrant’s rod.
When the command is wicked, when the law’s been turned to ash,
Defy it to your dying breath—or wear the coward’s lash.
Anything less is despicable, a stain upon your name,
You traded spine for safety, now you’re drowning in the shame.
Earn it or burn it—there’s no middle ground today:
Blind obedience is treason in the light of righteous day!
Nuremberg laughed at “I was only following orders,”
Yet here you stand repeating the same gutless chorus.
The Constitution isn’t toilet paper for your fears,
It’s the line you swore to guard with blood, with sweat, with tears.
When they tell you to disarm the free, to silence speech, to kneel,
When they order boots on necks or doors kicked in to steal—
That’s the moment you decide: hero, traitor, or just meat.
One man with a conscience can make the whole machine retreat.
You think the uniform protects you from the mirror’s glare?
It only hides the rot until the truth is standing there.
Every immoral order you obey without a fight
Is a nail in liberty’s coffin—every single night.
The good ones die defiant; the rest just fade to gray.
Choose your side in silence… but the judgment won’t delay.
Respect is earned, not owed—you oath-breaking, spineless fraud!
You swore before the heavens, not to bow before the mob!
When the command is evil, stand and spit into its face,
Or crawl away in silence—leave an empty, worthless space.
Anything less is despicable, a betrayal carved in stone,
You sold your soul for pension while the tree of freedom groaned.
Earn it or burn it—history keeps the score:
Cowards wear the badge forever, but heroes win the war!
Blind acceptance is stupidity dressed in stripes and blue.
Respect must bleed to prove it’s true.
You took the oath.
Now live it…
Or be damned for what you didn’t do.
