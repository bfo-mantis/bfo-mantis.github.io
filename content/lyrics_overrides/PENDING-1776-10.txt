[Verse 1]
They march in step, they nod, they sign,
Ignorant officers with vacant eyes.
Corrupted judges trade their robes for gold,
Simple minds applaud the noose they tie.
Weakness wears a smile, calls itself polite,
Hides behind “I’m only trying to survive.”
Harmless hands that never dare to fight—
They grease the wheels while evil comes alive.
[Pre-Chorus]
They’re not the monster in the midnight tale—
They’re the neighbor who looks down and turns away.
Every shrug, every “not my place,”
Is the quiet vote that lets the tyrants stay.
[Chorus]
There’s no virtue in being harmless!
No halo for the coward who stays small!
The meek don’t inherit—they just enable
The hands of evil holding up the wall.
All it takes for darkness to devour the day
Is good men choosing weakness—choosing to obey.
In this broken, rotting system,
Tell me loud…
What will we allow?
What will we allow?
[Verse 2]
The question’s scrawled in ash across the square:
“What will be allow?” – the grammar bleeds despair.
Will we let the badge become a loaded gun?
Will we let the gavel crush what should be done?
Will the spineless scroll and sigh and hit “like,”
While chains are forged for every unborn son?
Harmless is the mask the tyrant loves to see—
A population trained to bend the knee.
[Pre-Chorus]
They whisper “peace” while sharpening the blade,
They call it safety while the free are flayed.
Every “I don’t want to rock the boat”
Is treason dressed in caution’s overcoat.
[Chorus]
There’s no virtue in being harmless!
No medal for the soul that never bites!
We were born with teeth, with fire, with a spine—
Time to show the world what danger ignites!
They are the hands of evil, soft and sure,
But we decide the ending of this war.
In this broken, corrupted system,
Scream it…
What will we allow?
What will we allow?
[Bridge – low, gravel-voiced, almost whispered at first, building]
The harmless man is dangerous in reverse—
He hands the whip, he tightens every curse.
The truly dangerous man stands in the square,
Looks evil in the eye and says, “Not there.”
Not my child.
Not my neighbor.
Not my line.
Draw it now.
Cross it and you’ll find…
A man who’s finally done pretending to be kind.
[Final Chorus – full roar, layered gang vocals]
There’s no virtue in being harmless!
Let the harmless rot in silence and in shame!
We are forged for danger, built to break the chain—
Virtue lives in courage, never in the tame!
They are the hands of evil, trembling, small—
But we are the storm that answers every call.
We decide—
What will we allow?
What will we allow?
NOTHING.
NOTHING.
We allow… NO MORE.
[Outro – slow, deliberate, echoing]
What will we allow?
What will we allow?
Be dangerous.
Be good.
Be unafraid.
There’s no virtue…
In being harmless.
Let it ring. The weak can keep their comfort.
The rest of us have work to do.
