In the hallowed halls where freedom’s architects once traced the divine plan,
I stand in awe of sovereign rights bestowed by heaven’s hand.
Self-evident truths, unalienable, carved against the tyrant’s sword,
Bill of Rights—the living covenant, our forefathers’ sacred word.
God-given liberty, the flame that no earthly power can tame,
Yet I feel the winds of erosion whispering the tyrant’s name.

The pillars shake, the foundations crack beneath the weight of silent schemes,
Rights once bright as morning now fade into twilight’s dying dreams.

Lord Almighty, hear my prayer from this troubled, yearning soul,
Ignite within my spirit’s core a fire that makes the broken whole.
Grant me righteous, iron resolve to battle tyranny’s advance,
To stand as sentinel for liberty, whatever be the chance.
To the bitter end I’ll fight it, come what may through storm and strife,
With faith unbreakable as oak and hope that cuts like knife.
My God-given sovereign freedom—I’ll defend it with my dying breath,
Restore these truths, O Righteous Judge, or grant me honored death.

I see the tongue of liberty bound by edicts from on high,
The arm to self-defend disarmed beneath a watchful, wary eye.
The sacred right to congregate now fractured by decree,
Petitions lost in labyrinths where justice used to be.
Privacy invaded, property seized in shadows without light,
Due process drowned in currents of an ever-growing might.
The dream our forefathers bled for frays at every seam and thread,
As new oppressors wear the masks where old ones once had bled.

O God who guided Washington through winter’s bitter vale,
Who strengthened Adams’ quill and Madison’s unyielding scale,
Look down upon this latter day with mercy and with might,
And forge within this humble heart the courage for the fight.
Not for vainglory do I plead, nor power’s fleeting throne,
But for the sacred trust of liberty that Thou hast made our own.
If the path demands my all, then let Thy will be done,
But steel my soul to never yield ’til the battle’s truly won.

So lift my voice to heaven’s throne in this, my solemn vow:
I’ll guard the Bill of Rights, my Lord, I will not falter now.
Unbreakable my faith in Thee, eternal is my hope,
To the bitter end, come what may—on freedom’s rugged slope.
For country forged in wisdom’s fire, for truths self-evident,
I’ll fight the tyranny that creeps until my strength is spent.
Amen.
