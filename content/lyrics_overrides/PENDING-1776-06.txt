[Verse 1]
We the People, forged in fire, not in chains,
Sovereign souls with ink-stained hands—
No crown, no throne, just parchment vows,
Rights unbowed, unbent, unbound.
From Lexington's echo to midnight's stand,
We claim what's ours by blood and sand.
Constitutional Republic—our shield, our sword!
Power leashed, not lord!
Free men rise, tyrants fall—
Tree of liberty drinks it all!
Blood of patriots, blood of kings,
Natural manure for freedom's springs!
Bill of Rights—our iron creed,
Speech unchained, faith unchained, arms decreed.
Government bows, it kneels, it serves—
Not master, but servant—hear the words:
We guard the flame, we water the root,
Rebellion's spark when tyrants loot.
Jefferson whispered through the storm:
"The tree must bleed, or it dies—
Patriots' veins, tyrants' lies."
So we stand, rifles raised, voices high—
No surrender, no compromise.
Constitutional Republic—eternal, alive!
Sovereign rights in every stride!
From sea to shining sea we swear:
Liberty's tree—we'll keep it there!
Refresh it fierce, refresh it true—
For me, for you, for freedom's crew!
