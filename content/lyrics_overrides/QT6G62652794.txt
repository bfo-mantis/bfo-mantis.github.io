What Is Tyranny?...

Tyranny is power gone rotten, no consent, no light,
When rulers serve themselves and crush every right.
Accountability dies, fear takes the wheel,
The law becomes a weapon — that’s the raw deal.
No more “we the people,” just “obey or else,”
Freedom gets buried while the tyrants help themselves.

WHAT IS TYRANNY?! Power without consent!
Rule by coercion, rights get bent!
When the state turns predator, not protector,
That’s tyranny — plain, loud, and clear!

They seize your cash, your car, your house on a hunch,
No conviction, no crime — just cops on the hunt.
Billions funneled straight to their budget each year,
You prove you’re innocent later if you can afford the tears.
Legal government theft with a badge and a smile,
That’s tyranny dressed up in “law enforcement” style.

“Shall not be infringed” — ironclad, unqualified command,
Bruen made it crystal — no balancing, no sleight of hand.
Background checks, bans, waiting lists — they call it “safety,”
But every restriction is a boot on our liberty.
Disarming the people so the rulers stay strong,
That’s tyranny trying to silence the song.

They cage you for plants, for powders, for choice,
Victimless crimes turned into mandatory voice.
Prisons packed wall-to-wall with bodies in orange,
Families shattered while the system keeps gorging.
Mass incarceration for things that hurt no one,
That’s tyranny hiding behind “just say no” slogans.

WHAT IS TYRANNY?! Power without consent!
Rule by coercion, rights get bent!
Forfeiture steals your stuff, guns get stripped away,
Drug war locks you up — that’s tyranny every day!

Three clear examples, no gray, no debate,
When the government preys on the free — that’s the state.
Now you know the name, now you see the game…
What is tyranny?
TYRANNY IS THIS!
And the tree of liberty is thirsty for change.
