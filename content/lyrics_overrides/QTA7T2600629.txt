(Verse 1 – slow, almost whispered, like wind through ruined columns)
Why do I raise the blade to thee,
O once-exalted, O eater of pleas?
Thy throne was built on marrow and screams,
A mountain of widows, a river of dreams
Drowned in the silt of thy decrees.
Now the sky remembers.
The earth turns cold beneath thy knees.

(Refrain – deliberate, echoing, as if spoken by many voices rising as one)
Why, tyrant, why dost thou beg for breath?
When breath was the coin thou denied the rest?
Mercy was a word thou never blessed—
Now it returns, a shadow, unconfessed.
No dawn shall pardon.
No star shall witness.
Only the dark shall drink this final jest.

(Verse 2 – slower still, each syllable drawn like a last heartbeat)
See how the ravens circle low,
Black petals falling on fields of woe.
Thy scepter splinters in trembling hands,
Thy purple robes drink the clotting sands.
Once thou wert thunder,
Now but a whisper in broken lands.
The chains thou forged now bite thine own wrists—
The harvest of cruelty ripens at last.

(Bridge – a long, measured pause after each line, atmosphere thickening, thunder distant but approaching)
I could have walked away.
I could have let the wind erase thy name.
But the graves still call.
The children still weep in forgotten flame.
The mothers still rock empty arms at night.
Their lullabies turned to ash and spite.
So ask me again, with thy trembling tongue—
Why cut thee down?
Why silence the song?

(Final Refrain – voices layered, growing darker, more inexorable, as the life ebbs)
No mercy comes riding on silver wings.
No oracle weeps.
No angel sings.
The air grows thick with the scent of iron,
The horizon swallows the last pale sun.
A life is taken.
A tyrant undone.
And the world exhales…
as if it had never begun.

(Coda – fading, almost a sigh into silence)
Why didst thou beg?
Because even tyrants fear the end.
But fear avails thee nothing, false king.
The blade remembers.
The night descends
as your final breath hangs in the air until the darkness claims it entirely.

(Sing it low. Let the pauses breathe like held breath before a storm. Let the final line hang in the air until the darkness claims it entirely.)
