What Is Rebellion?

Rebellion is the sovereign right of free men,
When tyranny becomes law, resistance kicks in.
Organized defiance, not random rage,
The last check on power when the system’s a cage.
Not riots for chaos, not violence for fun —
It’s duty awakened when consent is gone.

WHAT IS REBELLION?! The flip side of chains!
When the tree of liberty thirsts for blood in its veins!
Patriots and tyrants, the natural manure —
Rebellion’s the cure when the rulers get cruel!

Jefferson said it and lived it raw,
Throw off the kings, break every flaw.
“The tree of liberty must be refreshed from time to time
With the blood of patriots and tyrants — it’s natural manure, that line!”
Spirit of resistance, let them take arms,
A warning to rulers — stay clear of the harm.

Boston Tea Party — dumped the tax in the bay,
No voice, no consent? Then we don’t obey.
Lexington Green — shots heard round the world,
Militias stood tall when the redcoats unfurled.
Whiskey Rebellion, Shays’ uprising too,
Farmers said “enough” when the government screwed you.

WHAT IS REBELLION?! The sovereign right to fight!
When tyranny rules, we answer with might!
Not chaos, not vengeance — just freedom’s last stand,
We water the tree with a defiant hand!

So when the boot comes down and the law turns to chain,
Rebellion’s not treason — it’s freedom’s refrain.
Now you know the name, now you feel the flame…
What is rebellion?
REBELLION IS THIS!
And the tree drinks deep once again.
