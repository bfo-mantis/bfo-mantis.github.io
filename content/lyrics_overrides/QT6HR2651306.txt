In the silence before the first dawn's cruel break,
Where chaos bowed low and the void learned to shake,
The Creator etched fire in the soul of each man—
Life unassailable, liberty's span.
Not granted by kings with their blood-stained decrees,
Nor parliaments bloated on stolen decrees.
These rights burn eternal, unborrowed, unbent,
God-endowed thunder no tyrant can rent.

From dust we were spoken, with dominion inside,
Not serfs for the system, not cogs to abide.

Sovereign I stand, on the altar of grace,
Where chains turn to ashes and cowards lose face.
Liberty surges from Heaven's own vein,
Not bureaucrat's ledger, not collective's refrain.
Father, ignite me—cut tyranny's throat,
Let the sword of my will meet the shield of Your oath.
This blood is Your image, this breath is Your claim—
I bow to no master but the One who enflamed.

They peddle dependence like venom in veins,
Redefine freedom as leashes and chains.
Collectivist altars where souls are erased,
While the State plays Messiah in power's embrace.
But the bone-deep truth slices empires to dust:
Rights aren't invented—they're holy, they’re just.
From Eden's first garden to Concord's first shot,
Sovereignty whispers: "You were never their lot."

Oh Ancient of Days, sear this cowardice out,
Strip every illusion that bondage is safe.
When the mob screams for masters and darkness cries loud,
Remind me my anchor is nailed to Your name.
No vote can revoke what Your finger inscribed,
No law can extinguish what eternity signed.

Sovereign we rise, from the grave of consent,
Where the governed remember what Heaven first meant.
Liberty roars from the throne of the King,
Not the fist of the many, nor the crown of the ring.
Cut to the marrow, ignite every heart—
This prayer is a war cry, this ballad a spark.
God-endowed fury, unyielding, untamed—
I am sovereign forever... in Your holy name.

Not from man... but from Thee.
