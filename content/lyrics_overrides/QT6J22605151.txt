[Intro]
Words mean things. Facts are stubborn things.
You trade liberty for the soft promise of safety
and wake up owning neither. Freedom is scary.
It sure as fuck isn’t safe.

[Verse 1]
They smile with the clipboard and the quiet blue eyes,
“I’m from the government and I’m here to help…”
Help you die. Slow. Soft. One cut at a time.
Death by a thousand cuts dressed up as care.
Safety is the new leash. Security the new cage.
They call it protection while they measure the chain.
You give them the key “just this once” for the greater good
and the road to hell opens under your feet like a trap door.
Good intentions pave every brick.
They never tell you the destination is the same every time.

[Chorus]
The road to hell is paved with good intentions,
soft words that bury the bodies under paperwork.
Safety equals control. Help equals kill.
Liberty for temporary quiet and you lose both.
Freedom is scary. It sure as fuck isn’t safe.
But the alternative is a velvet noose
and a thousand smiling cuts that never quite bleed enough
to wake the room.

[Verse 2]
They renamed the knife.
Abortion isn’t pro-choice. It’s feticide.
Call it what it is when the smallest human
is reduced to a choice and a clinic schedule.
Words mean things. Stop polishing the blade.
Gun safety never made a single outlaw safer.
Only the law-abiding lose the means to answer.
When the state alone holds the trigger
the quiet ones learn what helplessness tastes like.
Facts are stubborn. They don’t care about your feelings
or the press release that says this time it’s different.

[Chorus]
The road to hell is paved with good intentions,
soft words that bury the bodies under paperwork.
Safety equals control. Help equals kill.
Liberty for temporary quiet and you lose both.
Freedom is scary. It sure as fuck isn’t safe.
But the alternative is a velvet noose
and a thousand smiling cuts that never quite bleed enough
to wake the room.

[Bridge]
I’m from the government and I’m here to help… you die.
Slow enough that you thank us for the mercy.
Death by a thousand cuts.
Each one justified. Each one temporary.
Each one necessary. Until the only freedom left
is the freedom to obey.
Anyone willing to trade liberty for security
deserves the quiet that follows.
And the quiet is permanent.

[Verse 3]
They speak in inverted light.
Progress that levels everything taller than the median.
Equity that punishes the unequal until nothing stands.
Inclusion that excludes the ones who still remember
what the words used to mean.
Public health that treats the body as state property.
Stability that requires silence.
Care that arrives with handcuffs and a form to sign.
The rhetoric is the anesthetic.
The intention is the blade.
You feel the cut only after the smile has left the room.

[Final Chorus / Outro]
The road to hell is paved with good intentions
and the pavement is still warm from the last ones who believed.
Safety equals control. Help equals kill.
Words mean things. Facts are stubborn things.
Freedom is scary. It sure as fuck isn’t safe.
But the alternative is a velvet noose
and a thousand smiling cuts
that never quite bleed enough
to wake the room…
until the room is already gone.

I’m from the government
and I’m here to help
you die.
