In shadowed halls where tyrants scheme,
A sentinel stands, unyielding dream,
With eyes like eagles, piercing night,
He guards the spark of freedom's light.
Through tempests wild and battles fierce,
His heart a forge, his will a pierce,
No slumber claims his weary frame—
He tends liberty's eternal flame.

Oh, rise, ye guardians of the free!
With blood and vow, eternally,
We pay the price in sweat and tear,
To keep the blaze forever near.
Sacrifice calls, vigilance reigns,
Through duty's vow, we shatter chains!
For liberty's fire, we stand as one,
Unyielding till the dawn is won!
From ancient fields where heroes fell,
To whispered oaths in prison cells,
The mothers weep, the fathers bleed,
Sowing the soil with valor's seed.
No crown of gold, no fleeting fame,
Just endless watch o'er sacred name—
Against the winds of doubt and blame,
They tend liberty's hallowed flame.

Oh, rise, ye guardians of the free!
With blood and vow, eternally,
We pay the price in sweat and tear,
To keep the blaze forever near.
Sacrifice calls, vigilance reigns,
Through duty's vow, we shatter chains!
For liberty's fire, we stand as one,
Unyielding till the dawn is won!
When shadows creep and embers fade,
The call resounds, undismayed:
Awake, arise, with thunderous might,
Rekindle hope in endless fight.
For every scar, a story told,
Of tyrants broken, spirits bold—
Through vigilance, the tale unfolds,
We tend liberty's flame of gold.

Oh, rise, ye guardians of the free!
With blood and vow, eternally,
We pay the price in sweat and tear,
To keep the blaze forever near.
Sacrifice calls, vigilance reigns,
Through duty's vow, we shatter chains!
For liberty's fire, we stand as one—
Eternal vigil, the battle never done!....
