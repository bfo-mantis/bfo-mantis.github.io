[Title: Sovereign Righteous Free Men]

[Intro / Verse 1]  
In the year of our Lord seventeen seventy-six, the call went out across the colonies,  
Embattled farmers left their plows to answer destiny.  
Against the intolerable acts that sought to crush their will,  
Righteous rebellious free man rose upon the highest hill.  
They declared the rights endowed by their Creator's hand,  
Brothers united under God to defend this sacred land.  
Blazing stars would light the way through the smoke of musket fire,  
Freedom isn't free — it demands the ultimate desire.  
Come what may, they pledged it all, their fortunes and their lives,  
For the liberty within that in their hearts forever thrives.

[Chorus]  
Oh righteous rebellious free man, brothers united we stand tall,  
One nation under God, answering freedom's solemn call.  
Blazing stars and stripes unfurled against the stormiest sea,  
Do not tread on me — this is our God-given sovereignty.  
Come what may through fire and flood, through every darkened hour,  
We celebrate two hundred fifty years of liberty's great power.  

[Verse 2]  
From Lexington Green where the shot heard round the world was fired,  
To the winter at Valley Forge where hope itself was tried.  
They crossed the Delaware in ice, with courage as their guide,  
Embattled farmers turned the tide on the opposing side.  
Intolerable acts could never quench the fire in their souls,  
For they fought for something greater than kings and their controls.  
Brothers united, marching on with prayers upon their lips,  
Building the foundation where true liberty equips.

[Chorus]  
Oh righteous rebellious free man, brothers united we stand tall,  
One nation under God, answering freedom's solemn call.  
Blazing stars and stripes unfurled against the stormiest sea,  
Do not tread on me — this is our God-given sovereignty.  
Come what may through fire and flood, through every darkened hour,  
We celebrate two hundred fifty years of liberty's great power.  

[Verse 3]  
Through the ages that have followed, the spirit never died,  
From the halls of Montezuma to the shores where heroes cried.  
In every generation, the call to stand rings true,  
Righteous rebellious free man, me and you.  
The fields once stained with blood now bloom with harvest gold,  
Stories of the fallen in our hearts forever told.  
Freedom isn't free, the lesson echoes down the years,  
Through joy and through the sorrow, through laughter and through tears.

[Bridge]  
Come what may when new threats rise to test our sacred ground,  
We remember embattled farmers and the stands that they have found.  
Do not tread on me is more than just an ancient cry,  
It's the beating heart of freedom beneath the open sky.  
Brothers united, hand in hand, one nation under God,  
We lift our voices higher on this consecrated sod.  
Blazing stars will guide our path no matter what the cost,  
For the liberty within us cannot ever be lost.

[Verse 4]  
Two hundred fifty years of sovereign righteous might,  
From the Revolution's dawn to this very shining night.  
We honor every sacrifice that made our freedom real,  
The price paid by the many so the dream would never kneel.  
Raw and powerful emotions surge when we recall the fight,  
Gripping souls and stirring hearts beneath the beacon light.  
In cities, towns, and prairies wide, the spirit still remains,  
Sovereign righteous free men, guardians of the flames.

[Verse 5]  
Today we raise our banners high, celebrating this great span,  
From thirteen colonies to this mighty sovereign land.  
The intolerable acts of old are lessons we still heed,  
Lest we forget the blood that bought the freedom that we need.  
Brothers united in the cause, under God's almighty grace,  
We stand as one against the tide, in every time and place.  
Come what may, the torch is passed to every new-born son,  
The righteous rebellious free man spirit marches ever on.

We celebrate two hundred fifty years of liberty's great power.  
Soul-stirring, raw and gripping, the anthem in our veins,  
Sovereign righteous free men breaking every tyrant's chains.  
Brothers united, blazing stars above us ever shine,  
Freedom isn't free, but in our hearts it burns divine.  
One nation under God, come what may we hold the line,  
Righteous rebellious free man — America divine.  
In fields where embattled farmers once took their righteous stand,  
The spirit of the free man echoes across this mighty land.  
Come what may, we hold the line with faith that will not break,  
One nation under God, for all our children's sake.  
Blazing stars will light the night, guiding us come what may...
