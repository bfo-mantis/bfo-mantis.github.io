[Intro]
Mine eyes have seen the glory of liberty unchained,
A thunder rolling westward where the tyrants are arraigned.
From the smoke of ancient musket to the spark that never dies—
The tree of freedom stands eternal, reaching to the skies!
[Verse 1]
In the fat years of the feast, the weak grow soft and blind,
They trade their birthright for the glitter, leave their honor far behind.
Comfort wraps them like a shroud, they kneel before the throne,
Till the storm breaks on their doorstep, and the seeds of wrath are sown.
But hard times forge the faithful, in the crucible of pain,
Strong men rise from shattered fields, blood and fire in their veins.
Embattled farmers at the green, with powder black and true,
Faced the redcoats' iron heel—Intolerable, through and through!
[Pre-Chorus]
We hold these truths as blazing stars, self-evident and bright:
Life and liberty and happiness—endowed by Heaven's might!
No king, no council, no decree shall strip what God has given—
The Bill of Rights, our iron creed, our sword forever driven!
[Chorus]
Glory, glory, hallelujah!
Liberty's thunder, crashing through ya!
Glory, glory, hallelujah!
The tree drinks deep from patriot blood—rise up, rise up, the cycle turns!
Strong men rise! Weak men burn!
Break the yoke, let freedom roar—forevermore!
[Verse 2]
The wheel keeps turning, brothers, good times follow the fight,
But vigilance must guard the flame through every endless night.
Weakness creeps like creeping rust when the battles fade away,
Till the tyrants rise in velvet gloves, and steal the light of day.
Now in this hour of shadowed power, mandates cold and sly,
Surveillance eyes and silenced cries—awake, or liberty will die!
From Concord bridge to city streets, the rebel spirit calls,
Reclaim the rights they dare infringe—stand tall, or watch them fall!
[Pre-Chorus]
The right to bear our arms shall stand, untrammeled, unafraid,
To speak, to gather, to defy—the bands of bondage frayed!
When government turns destroyer of the ends for which it rose,
The people have the sacred right—to alter, to oppose!
[Chorus]
Glory, glory, hallelujah!
Liberty's thunder, crashing through ya!
Glory, glory, hallelujah!
The tree drinks deep from patriot blood—rise up, rise up, the cycle turns!
Strong men rise! Weak men burn!
Break the yoke, let freedom roar—forevermore!
[Bridge]
Feel the lightning in your chest, the fateful swift and sure,
A gospel writ in burnished steel, the serpent crushed once more!
No more the kneeling, no more the chain—defiance in our cry,
The Hero born of woman leads us onward to the sky!
[Encore Chorus]
Glory, glory, hallelujah!
Liberty's thunder, crashing through ya!
Glory, glory, hallelujah!
The tree of liberty must stand—its roots in righteous blood!
Rise now, in this crucial hour, seize the flame before the flood—
The moral rings like judgment's bell: freedom's not a gift, it's won by those who rebel!
Strong men guard the sacred fire—or tyranny will fell us all!
[Outro]
Mine eyes have seen the glory…
His truth—our liberty—is marching on!
Glory, glory… hallelujah!
