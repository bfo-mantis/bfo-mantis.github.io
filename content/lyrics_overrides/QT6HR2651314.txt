Before the throne, unblinking, I empty out my name
The coal that seared Isaiah’s mouth is reaching for the same
I carried alibis like incense — cheap, already spent —
But holiness will not make peace with anything I meant
His word falls like a mason’s hammer on my gilded lies
The shelter I called safety is the room where I die.
Yet in the breaking there is bread, and in the wound a well
The Judge who reads my ruin is the Christ who stormed my hell
I wore Pharaoh’s iron, Babel’s mark, burned into my kin —
River-mud still on my ankles, tower-perfume on my skin
I learned their lullabies by heart and called the cage my rest
Until the Rider spoke a sentence and the dark confessed
Now the lock is only rumor. The grave has lost its key
What the Lamb wrote open, death itself cannot unsee
I do not stand in borrowed iron. I stand inside Your will
The heart is Yours. The fire is Yours. The dark cannot kill

This is not a mood I hired. This is war I did not start
The Kingdom is not later. It is bursting in the heart

Sovereign Heart of Fire!
Take the throne I kept!
Burn the counterfeit devotion and the comfort where I slept!
Living hope that will not buckle — love that split the tomb,
Faith that faces mountains and denies them any room
Liberty is blood upon the door
Every chain that learned my language learns it nevermore

In the cut where midnight still swears it owns the road,
I set my boots on purchased ground, on mercy never owed —
Yet mercy poured until the ledger screamed enough
The Accuser drafts his treaties in the margins of my rough
I do not sign. The wood already answered every clause
Hope is not a pretty wish — it’s iron in the pit’s own jaws
Love is not a warmth. It is a blade that learned to sing
Faith is not a closed-eye leap. It is sight of the true King
Seated. Speaking. Finished. I will not rent the dusk,
Or lease a borrowed kingdom, or crown a lesser lord
I was purchased for a freedom that will never bow or deal
With the grave, or gild the cage, or give the dark our seal
This hope is not a trinket. This hope is not a charm
This love’s a blood-oath hell cannot afford to harm

I remember dressing bondage in the language of the wise,
Calling silence a discretion, calling sleep a compromise
I remember feeding idols just to keep the night from noise,
Trading thunder for a whisper so the fear would call it peace
Then the fire did not visit. It took residency inside.
The Sovereign sat where I had sat. I was no longer guide
The ribcage that had hidden dross became an altar-stone
The voice I used to bury is the voice that took the throne
I will not crawl to Egypt for a bowl of yesterday,
Nor rebuild the pretty locks my freedom tore away
If the wasteland is His kiln, this fire is not a private flame
Drown the old man in the flood. We rise beneath one Name

Let the fire fall doubled — mercy’s oil and judgment’s coal
Peel the gold-leaf. Let the idol show its hole
No velvet intercession. No tactful, tasteful flight
We watch the captives dancing on the ruins of the night
We are the answer to the groan that rose from every land —
Unbought, unbent, unburied, with no white flag in the hand
Let the Kingdom come like water when the river takes the stones —
Not a trickle for the tender — a flood on slums and thrones
Let the buried stand and speak. Let the remnant raise the sign
We will not kiss the curse for calm to keep our trembling still
We will not trade this fire for balm, or bow to lesser will

Sovereign Heart of Fire!
Take the people, take the throne!
Till every fearful chest is kindled to the bone!
Living hope that will not buckle — love that split the tomb,
Faith that faces mountains and denies them any room
The Son has torn the sentence. We are free. He rose
From the marrow to the far ends of the earth,
We roar until the dark has nothing left of worth

We rise from the ashes, not as dust
Never saved for softness. Saved to break the gates and thrust
His Kingdom through the dark.
Sovereign Heart of Fire…
Let the bound ones stand…
