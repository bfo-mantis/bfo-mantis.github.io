Liberty's Eternal Flame

bfo.mantis

  1. 30-second preview
ListenPre-save / listen (opens in a new tab)

Lyrics

Our rights are sovereign, from God they flow, Not granted by kings or the government show. The Bill of Rights secures what’s always been ours, Defend every one with unyielding power. No erosion, no compromise, liberty’s call, Stand firm for freedom, or watch freedom fall. When government turns corrupt and unjust, It’s our duty to rebel — in God we trust! First Amendment, five freedoms shine bright: Religion unhindered, worship as is right. Speech without shackles, say what you believe, Press free to question, the truth to achieve. Assemble in peace with your brothers and kin, Petition the rulers, let grievances win. Second Amendment keeps us secure and free, Right to bear arms for our liberty. Against tyrants and thieves who would do us harm, This God-given right sounds the liberty alarm. Third says no soldiers quartered in your abode, Without your consent, on that sacred code. Fourth guards your home from searches unwise, Unreasonable seizures under government guise. Warrant and cause, probable and true, Protects your effects, persons, papers too. Fifth Amendment stands for justice fair: Grand jury indictment, no double jeopardy snare. No forced confession, due process for all, Eminent domain with just payment call. Sixth for the accused: speedy public trial, Impartial jury from the local file. Face your accusers, witnesses testify, Counsel to defend you — the truth won’t die. Compulsory process to bring forth your proof. Seventh ensures jury trials in civil suits, Common law facts preserved, resolving disputes. Eighth prohibits excessive bail and fines, Cruel punishments that cross moral lines. Ninth declares rights not all enumerated here, Retained by the people, forever near. Tenth reserves powers not given to the feds, To states or people, where sovereignty spreads. So memorize them, teach your children well, These chains on power, these liberty bells. From God they come, eternal and grand, We the People must guard this promised land. No compromise on rights God did bestow, Rebel if needed when corruption grows. Alter or abolish, rebuild anew — A government of, by, and for me and you!
