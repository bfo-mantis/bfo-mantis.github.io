Lyrics

OPPRESSION! They built the walls with stolen breath, Wrote the laws in blood and debt. Told the herd to kneel and pray To the gods they forged from clay. But in the ash of broken chains One man stands, no crown, no name— Sovereign under God alone, Unbowed, unbought, unbroken bone. Hear the thunder in his chest, The last free heartbeat left. No army at his back, Just the fire they can’t extract. One man! One righteous, rebellious, free man! Heart unconquered, fist still raised, DEFIANT in the tyrant’s face. One man under Heaven’s eye, Standing when the cowards die. And if you feel that fire start— You are that one man in your heart. They’ll call him traitor, madman, fool, Hang his name with every tool. Starve his children, burn his land, Offer silver for his hand. Still he answers with a roar That shakes the foundations of their war: “I was born free—I’ll die the same. You own the cage. You never owned the flame.” Feel the iron in his blood, The sacred, savage flood. No flag can claim his soul, No king can make him whole. One man! One righteous, rebellious, free man! Heart unconquered, fist still raised, DEFIANT in the tyrant’s face. One man under Heaven’s eye, Standing when the cowards die. And if you feel that fire start— You are that one man in your heart. They fear the solitary spark More than legions in the dark. Because one man who will not kneel Can teach a million how to feel. So when they come with chains and lies, Look them dead between the eyes— And speak the only oath that lasts: “I AM FREE! I HOLD THE FIRE IN MY HEART OF THUNDER! You will not pass!” ONE MAN! ONE RIGHTEOUS, REBELLIOUS, FREE MAN! HEART UNCONQUERED, FIST STILL RAISED, DEFIANT IN THE TYRANT’S FACE! ONE MAN UNDER GOD ALMIGHTY, STANDING FOR THE LIGHT THEY’RE FIGHTING! AND IF YOU FEEL THAT FIRE START— YOU ARE THAT ONE MAN… YOU ARE THAT ONE MAN… YOU ARE THAT ONE MAN IN YOUR HEART! One sovereign free man under God Almighty Makes a difference. Become that One Man in your heart of THUNDER!!!.. Thunder... Thunder. Burn bright!
