One Man Nation (Under God)

bfo.mantis

More about this track

A spoken prayer that closes the album by calling each free person a nation unto himself, sovereign under God and armed. It widens the album's single heroes into a vision of a whole people.

Lyrics

O God Almighty, Sovereign of the ages, Hear the cry of freeborn souls who will not kneel. We stand as one man, each alone beneath Your throne, Yet bound together by the blood and iron of Your will. You breathed into dust and made a living spirit; You set the solitary on high and crowned him king of his own domain. No mob may claim him, no majority devour him, For the individual is Your image, unalienable, eternal, unbroken. One Man Nation — under God! One Man Nation — under God! Half a billion rifles flash like judgment in the dawn; Half a billion hearts beat with the thunder of Your law. We are the army You assembled when the world was still in chains; We are the light that burns the darkness back into the void. From the first shot at Lexington to the last stand at the Altar of Liberty, History testifies: one man under heaven can move mountains. One farmer with a musket. One preacher with a Bible. One soldier with a conscience. Miracles are not the work of crowds; they are the work of the solitary soul Who answers only to the living God. We are that soul multiplied across a continent, Each man a fortress, each man a flame, each man a sovereign. Those who conspire in secret chambers, Who draft the chains in soft language and soft offices, Who would trade the birthright of free men for the comfort of the managed herd — Hear us now. Your systems rot where they touch the open sky. Your power ends where a free man’s finger rests upon the trigger of his birthright. You cannot outnumber the ones who stand alone with God; You cannot outgun the nation that keeps half a billion reasons to remain free. One Man Nation — under God! One Man Nation — under God! We do not beg for permission. We do not petition for our rights. We already possess them, sealed in blood and written in the stars. Give us, O Lord, the metal — cold, sharp, and pure — To cut the sickness from our own house. The sickness that whispers “safety first,” The sickness that trades courage for comfort, The sickness that abandons the altar and kneels before the state. Let the blade of conviction sever every root of tyranny within us. Let no soft heart remain where steel is required. Let no compromise live where Your truth has spoken. We are not a democracy of the trembling; We are a republic of the armed and the faithful. One man stands above the mob because You placed him there. One man refuses the yoke because You broke it on the cross and at Concord. United we rise as Your army, not by numbers alone, But by the unbreakable chain of individual conviction. Each soul a sovereign. Each rifle a declaration. Each heartbeat a battle cry that shakes the foundations of every empire built on fear. Nothing in this fallen world can stand against this righteous light Except the cancer that grows when we forget Your name. So we pray: burn it out. Cut it clean. Leave only the living tissue of liberty. One Man Nation — under God! One Man Nation — under God! We are the greatest power ever assembled under heaven — Not because we march as one body, But because each of us marches as a nation unto himself, Answerable to none but the Almighty. Let the enemies of freedom hear the roar: We will not be ruled. We will not be managed. We will not be disarmed. We will not abandon the God who made us free. Rise, O Sovereign souls. Stand, O solitary kings. Fight, O army of the One Man. For the day is coming when the last chain snaps And the last conspirator learns the meaning of a free people under God. Amen. And amen. And let the mountains echo it forever. One Man Nation — under God.
