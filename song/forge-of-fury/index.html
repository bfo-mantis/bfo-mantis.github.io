Forge of Fury

bfo.mantis

  1. 30-second preview
ListenPre-save / listen (opens in a new tab)

Lyrics

Oh Sovereign Lord, in this darkening hour, The chains of tyrants tighten 'round the free, They feast on flesh while liberty devours, Their thrones of lies built on our broken knees. I see the storm that must consume the night, The blood that has to spill to make us whole, No path but fire, no dawn without the fight— Lord, give me steel to pay this heavy toll. Strike me down if mercy fails the blade, I'll lay my life upon the altar stone, With righteous fury, let the price be paid, For every soul the tyrants turned to bone. I pray for them—the corrupted, blind, and damned— Even as I prepare to crush them where they stand. God of Justice, hear this warrior's cry, Make me Your hammer… or better yet, reply. They were once children laughing in Your light, Now hollow kings with hearts of rust and rot, They steal our breath, they murder what is right, And call their evil "order" while we rot. Forgive them, Father, for they know not what— Or maybe they do, and chose the serpent's tongue. Still I pray as I chamber up the shot, Have mercy on the wicked when it's done. Lord, answer not with one man's death alone, Not with my bones scattered on the field. Touch the millions sleeping in their homes, Ignite their veins with holy fire unsealed! Let the meek arise like lions in the street, Let quiet fathers turn to swords of flame, Let mothers roar, let children leave their feet— And every tyrant hear their numbered name! Then strike them down with fury none can stay! A million hands united in Your name! The righteous rebel, washing sin away, With sovereign slaughter—purging out the shame! We pray for mercy, yet the blade replies, The wicked fall like wheat before the tide. Let heaven thunder while Your people sing, Liberty reborn from tyrants' blood and pain! Let the world rejoice where the monsters die, No more their boot upon the human neck. Though we lay our lives down, we'll rise unified, Your answer written in their broken necks. From ashes rises freedom's holy roar, The corrupted gone—forever, evermore. I offer You my life… send us this war, And in that war, we'll stand forevermore. Righteous fury… sovereign fury… Let the people rise… let tyrants fall… God will answer… and all shall be well.
