Lyrics

The sky holds its breath... the charge remembers its own name... Pressure gathers where the old orders stand guard, Veils of habit press the air until it hardens hard. We move through corridors someone else designed, Trading breath for safety, sight for being blind. The storm begins inside the quiet we defend, A low vibration rising where the false peace ends. What we called freedom was the space between the bars, What we called power was surrender wearing stars. A crack splits open in the vault we kept so still... The voltage finds the path it always meant to fill... Sovereign thunder, roll through the decree, Liberty answers when the storm is set free. No longer waiting for permission from the throne, The will that shakes the heavens claims its own. Strike every silence that kept the old world whole, Sovereign thunder answers from the center of the soul. In the heart of the tempest the ancient walls give way— True liberty stands when we no longer look away. The strike arrives without announcement or request, Illusion shatters in the moment it is pressed. What once was scattered gathers into one clear voice, The scattered pieces recognize they had no choice. Authority returns to where it always slept, Not granted from above but from the vow we kept. The storm no longer something we endure or flee— It is the force we ride when we decide to be. We thought the thunder came to tear the kingdom down, Yet in its voice we heard the kingdom’s only crown. The power that can break is also power that can hold, The same charge that awakens is the charge that makes us whole. No separation left between the storm and sovereign will— The one who stands inside it is the one who stands there still.
