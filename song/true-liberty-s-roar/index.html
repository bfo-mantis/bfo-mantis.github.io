Lyrics

In the court where the Ancient of Days weighs every hidden thing, My soul is laid bare beneath the scepter of the Sovereign King. No defense left, no clever disguise — Righteous conviction cuts straight through the lies. It burns like holy acid through the dross in my chest, Exposes every chain I once called “rest.” Yet mercy meets judgment at the throne of fire, And the same hand that breaks me is the hand that lifts me higher. I was born in bondage, Egypt in my blood, Babylon whispering comfort in the mud. But the Lion roars my name across the veil, And the grave itself cannot reverse what He has sealed. So I stand — not in strength, but in surrendered steel, Heart convicted, covenant real. This is not emotion, this is war declared, Not a feeling — it’s the Kingdom coming near. Sovereign righteous, convict my heart! Burn till nothing false remains in the dark! Unbreakable hope, eternal flame, Love that conquered death and still bears my name. Faith that laughs when the mountains shake, True liberty fulfilled — every chain must break! We rise from the ashes, we roar from the dust, Not for comfort — for the Kingdom’s thrust! Through the valley where the shadows still pretend they own the night, I plant my feet on blood that purchased every right. The enemy offers treaties written in my shame, But the cross already answered every claim. Hope is not a wish — it’s the anchor dropped in hell, Love is not a feeling — it’s the sword that breaks the spell. Faith is not blind — it sees the throne room clear, And knows the Sovereign’s “yes” is already here. So I refuse the half-light, the lukewarm throne, The chains that look like safety when you’re still alone. I was bought for freedom, not to negotiate with graves, Not to bow to lesser kings or decorate my cage. This conviction is a furnace, this hope a blazing sword, This love a covenant that hell cannot afford. Let the fire fall like Pentecost and judgment at the same time, Let conviction thunder till the idols lose their shine. No more silent prayers, no more polite retreat — We are the generation that will see the captive’s feet Dance on the ruins where the strongholds used to stand. We are the answer to the groan of every land. Unbreakable, unyielding, undefeated, unafraid, We carry liberty that cannot be delayed. So we cry it from the marrow, we decree it with our blood: Let Your Kingdom come — and let it come like a flood! Sovereign righteous, convict us all! Till every heart becomes a burning call! Unbreakable hope, love without end, Faith that endures when the world pretends. True liberty fulfilled — it is finished, it is done! The Son has set us free — we are free indeed, we run! From the depths of our soul to the ends of the earth, We roar it till the darkness has no more worth! Liberty fulfilled… In the name above every name… Liberty fulfilled… Let the captives rise… Liberty fulfilled… It is done. It is done. It is done. Amen.
