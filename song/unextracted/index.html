Unextracted

bfo.mantis

Unextracted is a taut rock anthem about the one thing no contract can take. The papers slide across the table, they price the breath and offer silver for the hand, but the pulse under the wrist keeps its own time. The song insists that pulse was never theirs to claim.

More about this track

Unextracted is set at a signing table, where the paper slides across the grain and the line waits for a name. They price the breath and offer silver for the hand, but the wrist stays still and the pulse keeps its own time. It beats where no one signed, and by the last chorus it's inside the skin, asking whether the same pulse answers in you.
