More about this track

Tree of Liberty rises from earth and clay, kin of the soil, blood and bone. Forefathers forged the seed in fire; now surveillance shadows choke the sky and twisted sophistry spins its lies. Then the scene moves into the forge, with a crucible, an anvil ringing true and bellows blasting an immortal spark. Home and hearth stand behind it, wolves and eagles stand united, and the bridge names the duty: guard the seed the fathers planted. Thy will be done.
