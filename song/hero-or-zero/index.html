Hero or Zero

bfo.mantis

More about this track

A rough-edged challenge to police who put their own safety above the people they serve, set against soldiers and others who run toward danger. It closes by honoring the ones who do the job right.

Pull up on a kid with a phone, now his mama got a shelf. One wrong move and you're ducking, retreating, radio screaming, While the paperwork's already drafted: "Feared for my being." Officer safety! That's the only hill you take, Qualified immunity—mistake? Nah, you're safe. Want a job where you never bleed, never break? Go work in a pillow factory, motherfucker, for fuck's sake! Soldiers storm the ridge, "Take that hill!"—yes sir! Cops roll up slow, "But sir, it might hurt..."—cancelling the tour. They cosplay as Rangers when the cameras flash bright, Tactical this, operator that, LARPing all night. But ask 'em to face real fire without backup or drone, They'll hide behind the badge and say, "I feared for my own." Soldier gets orders: "Go kill or be killed, move out!" Doesn't bitch about the odds, doesn't scream and pout. Charges the machine gun nest, bayonet fixed, eyes wide, While the cop tasers a grandma and claims he might've died. Now flip the script to the ones who do it right, Real warriors rise before the break of light. They don't wait for the green light or the safe zone call, They run to the danger, watch brothers fall. No qualified shield, no paid vacay escape, Just honor in the fight, for freedom's sake. They bleed for the Constitution, stand tall in the fray, Defend every right, come what may. But you?
