Intolerable

bfo.mantis

More about this track

Boston Harbor sits frozen and shut in Intolerable, with starvation on every quay after the Tea Party, town meetings gagged and redcoats quartered in private homes. Samuel Adams thunders, Joseph Warren pens the Resolves, Revere rides, and thirteen colonies meet as one in Philadelphia. Then the Acts are held up against today's punished cities and shielded officials, and the listener is called to stand like the patriots of '74, from Boston's ghosts to their own front door.

Lyrics

Boston Harbor lay frozen under Parliament’s cruel decree, Port Act slammed the gates shut—starvation on every quay. Tea Party lit the spark, but the Crown answered with chains, Five Intolerable Acts forged in London’s darkest flames. Charter ripped to ribbons, elected men cast aside, Town meetings gagged and broken, governors crowned with royal pride. Judges shipped across the sea where justice wore a British face, Quartered redcoats in our homes—tyranny in our own space. Samuel Adams thundered through the streets of midnight fire, Sons of Liberty rose like ghosts, fueled by righteous ire. Joseph Warren penned the Resolves—“No obedience is due!” One voice became a thousand, and the flame burned straight and true. Paul Revere rode the darkness, warnings cutting through the night, Minutemen grabbed their muskets, ready for the fight. They boycotted, they united, they refused the tyrant’s hand— From Boston’s broken harbor, defiance swept the land. This is the Intolerable Flame—burning hotter than their lies! We will not bow, we will not break, though the redcoats blacken the skies! One small stand in the dirt became the roar that shook the throne, Blood of patriots watering the tree that freedom calls her own. No king, no act, no iron boot can chain what God made free— We are the sons of seventy-four—untamed, wild, and free! Philadelphia called the Congress—thirteen colonies stood as one, Suffolk Resolves echoed loud: “These acts shall be undone!” They pledged their lives, their fortunes, their sacred honor on the line, Boycotts bit the British purse while the spirit rose like wine. From the ashes of oppression came the powder-keg of might, The shot heard round the world was born in that defiant night. Today the Acts wear different names—ports of livelihood slammed shut, Cities punished for their voice, rights stripped without rebut. Officials shielded from the law, troops of power in our streets, Foreign mandates in our homes where the Constitution bleeds. But the flame still burns the same—history screams the warning clear: When tyrants push too far, the people rise without fear! This is the Intolerable Flame—roaring louder than before! We will not bow, we will not break—liberty forevermore! From Boston’s ghosts to your front door, the call rings loud and true: Stand like the patriots of ’74—me and you! One life, one stand, one raging heart can turn the darkest tide, Water the tree with righteous blood—let freedom never die! No chains can hold what God set free—rise up and claim your name… The Intolerable Flame still burns—the Revolution lives again! Hear the hammer of the blacksmith… hear the hoofbeats in the night… The flame is in your blood tonight… Rise.
