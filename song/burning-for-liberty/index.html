In the shadow of chains where the timid bow low, Where the boot of the tyrant grinds hope in the dust, A spark in the breast of the righteous men glows— Sovereign souls forged in the fire of trust. No king, no committee, no cold iron throne Can bind what the Creator has breathed into bone. We feel it rising, the heat in our veins, The ancient call thundering through our names. Burning for Liberty! Flames that will not die! Stand, ye free men, under wide open sky! With hearts as our armor and truth as our blade, We rise like the dawn where the tyrants have laid. Let the old order tremble, let the liars take flight— For liberty’s fire is burning tonight! Burning! Burning! Let freedom reign anew, A downpour of justice in skies red and true! We are not serfs begging scraps from the state, Nor cogs in their engine of fear and control. Each man a kingdom, each soul a estate— With reason as scepter and courage as goal. From the ashes of history the lesson burns bright: Only free men can carry the torch of the right. The hour is late but the blood still runs hot, The spirit of ’76 never forgot. Burning for Liberty! Flames that will not die! Stand, ye free men, under wide open sky! With hearts as our armor and truth as our blade, We rise like the dawn where the tyrants have laid. Let the old order tremble, let the liars take flight— For liberty’s fire is burning tonight! Burning! Burning! Let freedom reign anew, A downpour of justice in skies red and true! No more kneeling. No more chains. No more silence while evil remains. Hand upon heart, eyes to the flame— We swear by the living: we’ll never be tame. Let it rain. Let it pour. Burning! Through night into day Liberty lives where free men stand and stay!
