They shot him seven times. Doctors wrote him off. Germans called him Ghost. He just kept coming back... Antifragile, motherfucker. Born in Buffalo, Polish blood in his veins, Cornell ROTC, turned pain into gains. North Africa sand, Sicily stealth in the night, Stealth and sweat over force—yeah, he taught 'em right. Bazooka in hand, two Panzer tanks in his sights, Hedgerows exploding, he lit 'em up twice. Leg shredded, arm hanging, they said "evac now," He laughed in their face— "I'm running this show." Shot through the neck, vocal cords torn like confetti, Last rites on the field, priest thought he was ready. Tracheotomy hack job, doctors bet he'd be dead, He woke up in England and stole a plane instead. Hitchhiked back to the front, limping like a demon, Mounted that Sherman, .50 cal screaming. Bullets ricocheting, he turned fear to fuel— While y'all fragile bitches break, he just got cruel. Antifragile! (Ghost!) Every hit makes me stronger, every wound makes me bolder. Antifragile! (Urban!) Seven Purple Hearts, still carrying the soldiers. You break, you bend, you cry for your mama— I get shot, I get up, I own the drama. Convex as fuck, volatility's my bitch, Matt Urban the Ghost—death couldn't finish this! Germany's finest tried to put him in the ground, Neck wound whispering, but he still made the sound. Led the charge anyway, grenades in the nest, Machine gun spitting while his blood painted the rest. Hospital bed? Nah, that's for the weak. He escaped like a specter, back in the heat. Commanded the battalion, chest full of shrapnel, Refused to leave 'til the objective was handled. They called him unkillable, a walking war crime, Nazis whispering "Geist" every damn time. Audie Murphy got fame, but Urban got seven, Most decorated ghost sending devils to heaven. Modern soft boys sipping oat milk and crying, He took tank rounds and kept the unit flying. Hormesis in hellfire—stress made him peak, You avoid the pain? You're already weak. They gave him last rites... he survived. Doctors said "no chance"... he thrived. Recommendation lost for decades... still arrived. Carter pinned the Medal— "Greatest soldier alive." Fragile systems shatter. Robust ones endure. Antifragile? They gain from the war. Antifragile! (Ghost!) Seven times down, eight times back on my feet. Antifragile! (Urban!) Turned every scar to a victory beat. You hide from disorder, you beg for the safe, I eat the chaos, I spit out the hate. Buffalo to Belgium, from grave back to glory— Matt Urban the Ghost wrote the antifragile story! They tried to kill the man... Created the legend instead. Antifragile till the end. The Ghost... still walks.
