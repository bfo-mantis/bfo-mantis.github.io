bfo.mantis

Songs for the one who will not kneel.

Bio

bfo.mantis believes in liberty, and that tyranny should be confronted wherever it's found. The songs carry that conviction across rock, metal, hip hop, folk and gospel: rights come from God, not the state, and a people united under God can't be broken. History supplies the heroes, from Thermopylae and Joan of Arc to Tiananmen Square and Ruby Ridge, alongside fierce attacks on surveillance, qualified immunity and central banking. Between the battle cries are tributes to mothers, brothers and anyone rebuilt by hardship.

Released independently on bfo.mantis Records since July 4, 2026.
