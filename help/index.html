Other ways to help

Every movement starts with one person who refuses to stay quiet. One share, one follow, one friend told about a song: that’s how a single voice becomes a crowd. You don’t need a stage or a fortune to stand with bfo.mantis. You just need to carry the signal one step further than it was.

Not every kind of support costs money. Here’s how to help get the word out and grow the community.

  • 01

    Follow

    Follow bfo.mantis where you listen, so new releases show up for you.

    • Apple Music (opens in a new tab)
    • YouTube Music (opens in a new tab)

    Save and add to playlists

    Saving songs and adding them to your playlists helps the most on streaming services.

    Subscribe

    Comment and like

    Leave a comment on YouTube and a like on the releases you enjoy. It means a lot.

  • 05

    Share

    Send the site to someone, or post it wherever you talk about music.

  • 06

    Tell a friend

    Know someone who would like these songs? Play them one next time you’re together.
