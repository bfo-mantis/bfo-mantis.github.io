DANGEROUS

bfo.mantis

DANGEROUS is built on one idea: a peaceful person who refuses to obey is far more powerful than a harmless one. Across 15 rock tracks, bfo.mantis takes aim at qualified immunity, unconstitutional laws, the language of official help and the people who enforce it, with history lessons from Marbury v. Madison to the 1946 Battle of Athens woven between the anger. The mood runs from stately hymns of vigilance to the most profane outbursts in the catalog, and every song comes back to the same choice between kneeling and standing.
