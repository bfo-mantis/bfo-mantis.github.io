Savage Serenade Volume 2

bfo.mantis

Savage Serenade Volume 2 is built on a single move: a quiet word placed in front of a violent one. Storms, fires and wild creatures are all softened in name, from Silent Tempest and Velvet Thunder to Soft Inferno and Quiet Claw, until Howling Calm and Tempests Lullaby turn the pattern around. The guitar traces a similar arc, starting with whispered single notes and glassy harmonics in what feels like a candlelit wooden room, then widening into a melancholy folk lament and a fast, thunderous climax before overdubbed guitars rise into a cathedral of sound. In the end it all thins out to one fading harmonic and silence, and the last title, Mellow Thunder, leaves the storm rumbling somewhere far off.

Tracklist

  1. 1
    Silent Tempest Instrumental

    A storm with the sound turned off opens the album, all of its force held in reserve. It sets the pattern the whole record follows: a gentle word standing guard over a violent one.

  2. 2
    Velvet Thunder Instrumental

    Thunder you could run your hand across, heavy but smooth. Second on the album, it keeps the opening hushed while hinting at how much weight is waiting.

  3. 3
    Gentle Savage Instrumental

    Here the wild creature itself is softened, the series name turned into a near-contradiction. Early in the record, it suggests a ferocity that has learned to be kind.

  4. 4
    Serene Storm Instrumental

    The calm that can live inside bad weather. As the album's opening stretch winds down, the title pictures turbulence watched from somewhere peaceful.

  5. 5
    Whispering Fury Instrumental

    Anger spoken under the breath, too low to hear but impossible to miss. Moving into the album's middle, it hints that the quiet is starting to strain.

  6. 6
    Soft Inferno Instrumental

    The first of the album's fire titles, a blaze described as if it were a blanket. It carries the record into its middle stretch, where warmth and loss begin to sit side by side.

  7. 7
    Tranquil Rage Instrumental

    Fury at rest but not gone. Near the center of the album, the title holds a feeling that has stopped shouting without ever letting go.

  8. 8
    Hushed Blaze Instrumental

    A fire burning without a crackle. The title continues the album's run of flames, keeping the heat close and the sound low.

  9. 9
    Peaceful Wildfire Instrumental

    A wildfire is the very picture of something out of control, and here it is called peaceful. At the album's midpoint, the title pushes its contrast about as far as it will go.

  10. 10
    Tender Maelstrom Instrumental

    A whirlpool that pulls you under gently. As the album turns from its middle toward its climax, the title imagines being swept away by something that means no harm.

  11. 11
    Still Flame Instrumental

    A single flame that doesn't flicker, the quietest fire on the record. It marks a pause in the album's second half, just before the energy gathers.

  12. 12
    Howling Calm Instrumental

    The album flips its own pattern: now the loud word describes the quiet one. Coming late in the record, the title suggests a calm so intense it seems to cry out.

  13. 13
    Quiet Claw Instrumental

    A claw drawn without a sound, the stillness of a predator before it strikes. Its place late in the album makes it feel like tension coiling toward release.

  14. 14
    Soft Savagery Instrumental

    The series name rewritten as a gentle force rather than a brutal one. Near the album's peak, the title keeps the wildness and takes the cruelty out of it.

  15. 15
    Serene Fury Instrumental

    Rage met with total composure. In the album's final stretch, the title sounds like a storm that has been absorbed rather than defeated.

  16. 16
    Whispering Inferno Instrumental

    A great fire speaking in a low voice, joining the album's whisper and flame images in one name. Late in the record, it gathers the earlier blazes into a single glow.

  17. 17
    Tempests Lullaby Instrumental

    The storm sings the listener to sleep, reversing the gentle-word-first pattern one more time. As the next-to-last track, it signals that the album is ready to rest.

  18. 18
    Mellow Thunder Instrumental

    The album closes much as it opened, with weather softened to a murmur. The final title leaves thunder rolling far away, still present but no longer a threat.
