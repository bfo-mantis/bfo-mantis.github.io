One Man INVICTUS

bfo.mantis

One Man INVICTUS is a gallery of individuals who changed events by refusing to bend, including the Tank Man of Tiananmen Square, Thich Quang Duc, Desmond Doss, Francis Marion, Gandhi, Joan of Arc and Leonidas. Nearly every one of its 15 rock tracks is titled One Man, and together they argue that history turns on single acts of conscience, peaceful or not. It ends in prayer, widening one person's stand into a nation of them.
