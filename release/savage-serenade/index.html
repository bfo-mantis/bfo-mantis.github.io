Savage Serenade

bfo.mantis

Savage Serenade asks one acoustic guitar to be two things at once. The titles spell it out, from Savage Surrender and Tender Carnage to Roses on the Blade and Edge of Softness, setting violence beside devotion in almost every name. The playing follows the same split: drifting harmonics and quiet classical lines break into blazing flamenco strumming and percussive tapping on the guitar body, then settle into a layered tremolo that slowly fades. It closes on Last Savage Prayer and Fiercely Grateful, where the fury and the tenderness finally sound like the same heart.

Tracklist

  1. 1
    Savage Surrender Instrumental

    The opener puts the album's contradiction right in its name: something wild that chooses to lay down its arms. As the first track, it sets the tone for a record where fierce and gentle are never far apart.

  2. 2
    Blood Kissed Instrumental

    A title that joins a wound to a kiss, as if tenderness and injury arrived in the same moment. Early in the album, it carries the opening's quiet forward with a darker, redder edge.

  3. 3
    Quiet Thorns Instrumental

    Thorns that make no sound: beauty with a hidden sting. It keeps the early part of the album soft on the surface while hinting that something sharper waits just underneath.

  4. 4
    Feral Edge Instrumental

    The first title on the album with no softer word to balance it, pointing to something untamed and sharp. It marks the record's turn from the hush of the opening toward wilder ground.

  5. 5
    Crimson Whisper Instrumental

    Crimson is the color of blood and passion, and here it arrives as a whisper rather than a shout. The title pulls the album back toward intimacy just after its first flash of wildness.

  6. 6
    Howling Grace Instrumental

    The cry of an animal set beside the poise of a prayer or a dancer. Closing out the album's first third, the title holds both without choosing between them.

  7. 7
    Velvet Ruin Instrumental

    Destruction wrapped in something soft enough to touch. In the middle of the album, the title suggests a collapse that comes slowly and almost gently.

  8. 8
    Tender Carnage Instrumental

    The starkest pairing on the record: slaughter described as tender. Sitting near the album's center, it states the theme of Savage Serenade as bluntly as any title here.

  9. 9
    Shards of Devotion Instrumental

    Devotion broken into pieces that can still cut. The title imagines a loyalty that survives being shattered, sharp-edged but still whole in its intent.

  10. 10
    Wild and Wanting Instrumental

    Hunger and wildness side by side, a title about desire with no leash on it. Just past the album's midpoint, it leans fully into the savage half of the record.

  11. 11
    Ashen Embrace Instrumental

    An embrace after the fire, holding on to what is left once everything has burned. The title brings warmth and devastation together in a single gesture.

  12. 12
    Silent Fury Instrumental

    Rage held in, with all its force and none of its noise. The title captures the album's habit of putting its fiercest feelings into its quietest words.

  13. 13
    Roses on the Blade Instrumental

    The album's clearest picture of its theme: a flower laid across a knife, love and violence touching. Coming late in the record, it gathers the opposites of every title before it into one image.

  14. 14
    Heart of the Storm Instrumental

    The still center inside the chaos, or the storm itself beating like a heart. As the album heads toward its climax, the title points to the place where the fury runs deepest.

  15. 15
    Edge of Softness Instrumental

    A mirror of Feral Edge from earlier in the record, this time giving the edge to gentleness. As the album nears its close, tenderness begins to take back ground.

  16. 16
    Echoes of the Wound Instrumental

    What lingers after the hurt has passed. Close to the end, the title looks back on the album's battles as something remembered rather than something still happening.

  17. 17
    Last Savage Prayer Instrumental

    Savagery and prayer meet one final time, as if the wildness itself has turned to devotion. As the next-to-last track, it begins the album's resolution.

  18. 18
    Fiercely Grateful Instrumental

    The album ends by giving its fierceness to gratitude instead of rage. The closing title makes peace between the two sides of Savage Serenade, ending on thanks said with full force.
