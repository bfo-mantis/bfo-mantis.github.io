Sovereign Heart of Fire

bfo.mantis

Sovereign Heart of Fire carries the catalog's language of liberty into worship. Its 15 Christian/Gospel songs move between prayers for the courage to defend God-given rights and quieter songs of surrender, grace and resilience, including the tender portrait Havilah. Fire runs through it as both judgment and renewal, and it closes with the singer handing the throne of his own heart to Christ.
