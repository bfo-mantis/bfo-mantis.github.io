Spellbound Volume 3

bfo.mantis

Spellbound Volume 3 tells a story of leaving and coming home, mostly in single words. It drifts off course with Wayward and Adrift, turns inward through Reflections, Resonance, Trance and Within, reaches Reunion and Bliss at its center, then cuts loose again with Untethered before Return brings it back and Last light and Flickering Glimpse let the light go out. The sound moves away from the first two volumes into slow, late-night lounge: a quiet heartbeat pulse, a deep double bass with warm walking lines and soft bowed notes, and wordless melodies that rise and fall like tides. It stays spacious and unhurried, with long silences between notes and a candlelit calm that settles over the record as it goes.

Tracklist

  1. 1
    Wayward Instrumental

    The album sets off by straying from the path, with no map and no hurry. As the opening title, it starts a journey that wanders before it ever finds its way home.

    More about this track

    The album opens with almost nothing: a slow heartbeat pulse and a deep double bass heard from a distance, its warm, woody walking lines wandering where they please. Wordless melodies drift in gradually, rising and falling like slow tides, slightly off-kilter and never quite where you expect. It is late-night lounge music that takes its time, the sound of someone happy to be a little lost.

  2. 2
    Adrift Instrumental

    No longer steering at all, just carried by the current. Second on the album, it takes the opening's wandering one step further out to sea.

    More about this track

    Adrift leans on the ocean in this sound. Soft bowed notes from the double bass swell and fade like a boat lifting on the swell, while the steady heartbeat underneath keeps time without ever pushing. Long silences open up between the melodies, so the music seems to float rather than move forward, carried wherever the current goes.

  3. 3
    Reflections Instrumental

    Still water and a long look back. With the drifting done, the album's third title turns the gaze inward.

    More about this track

    With Reflections the album settles into a candlelit room. Gentle reverb gives every note a soft shadow, and layered melodic lines rise and fall over the quiet pulse and the double bass's warm walking line, as if each phrase were being turned over and looked at again. The space between notes matters as much as the notes, inviting a slow, honest look back.

  4. 4
    Resonance Instrumental

    A note that keeps ringing after it is played, or a feeling that echoes in someone else. Early in the record, it finds something answering back from the quiet.

    More about this track

    Here the reverb and the deep, woody double bass do the talking, each low note lingering long enough to hum against the next. Precise, haunting melodic figures sound once and then melt into warmth, leaving their echo in the quiet. Over a heartbeat pulse that never hurries, the track feels built from what keeps ringing after the sound itself has gone.

  5. 5
    Trance Instrumental

    A word that links back to the enchantment of the first Spellbound volume. Near the album's middle, it marks the point where thought gives way to a slow, steady spell.

    More about this track

    The heartbeat rhythm becomes the center of Trance, a steady pulse that slowly pulls everything into its orbit. Hypnotic, slightly off-kilter melodies circle above the double bass, repeating and shifting just enough to hold attention without breaking the spell. Long-form and unhurried, it turns the album's calm into something close to meditation.

  6. 6
    Within Instrumental

    The deepest point of the journey inward. At the center of the album, the title looks for what was there all along.

    More about this track

    Within keeps the mood at its most private. Soft bowed bass notes and a quiet pulse sit close, like a heartbeat heard from the inside, while sparse melodies surface out of long stretches of silence. The candlelit warmth and gentle reverb make it feel like a room with the door shut, the place the album's journey inward has been heading.

  7. 7
    Reunion Instrumental

    After time apart, a meeting again. Just past the middle of the album, it is the first sign that the wandering is leading somewhere.

    More about this track

    Reunion lets layered melodies meet and intertwine over the double bass's patient walking line, like two voices finding each other after time apart. The heartbeat pulse stays steady and warm, and any edge in the precise, haunting phrases softens as soon as it appears. It is the calm of a long-awaited meeting, quiet and full at the same time.

  8. 8
    Bliss Instrumental

    The simplest, happiest word on the record, a moment of pure contentment. Placed right after Reunion, it lets the album rest in that feeling.

    More about this track

    In Bliss the album's soothing side comes to the front. Warm double bass, a slow, breathing pulse and floating melodies gather in candlelit reverb, with nothing hurried and nothing sharp left in the room. The silences feel restful rather than empty, and the long-form flow lets a single contented moment stretch out as long as it can.

  9. 9
    Last light Instrumental

    The final glow of the day before dark. In the album's last third, it brings a gentle sense that time is running short.

    More about this track

    Last light hears the same late-night palette as the sun going down. The double bass's low, woody tones deepen like evening shadows, while melodic lines rise and fall as if reluctant to fade. Gentle reverb and long pauses give each phrase a lingering glow, the feeling of watching the final color leave the sky.

  10. 10
    Untethered Instrumental

    Cut loose again, echoing Adrift from the album's opening. Late in the record, it suggests freedom rather than being lost.

    More about this track

    Untethered gives the melodies room to wander loose above the heartbeat pulse, slightly off-kilter and free of any obvious pattern. The double bass keeps a patient anchor underneath, warm and steady, so the floating lines can drift without getting lost. The spacious, unhurried flow sounds like freedom taken gently, a lightness that never tips into restlessness.

  11. 11
    Return Instrumental

    The journey turns for home. As the next-to-last title, it answers Wayward and closes the loop the album opened.

    More about this track

    Return circles back to where the journey began: a slow heartbeat, a deep double bass walking at its own pace, and wordless melodies rising and falling like the tides that carried the opening tracks away. Now candlelit warmth wraps around them, and the long-form flow feels less like drifting than coming home, closing the loop the album opened.

  12. 12
    Flickering Glimpse Instrumental

    The album ends on something barely seen, a light that comes and goes. The closing title leaves a brief, wavering image rather than a final answer.

    More about this track

    The album ends on brief, wavering moments: short melodic phrases that appear in the soft reverb and dissolve before they can settle, over a heartbeat pulse that keeps breathing to the end. The double bass's warm sustains hold the space between them as the silence grows, leaving the record on a faint, flickering image, calm but not quite still.
