Crown of Ash

bfo.mantis

Crown of Ash is a slow, solemn reckoning sung to a fallen tyrant in the style of an old ballad. The narrator weighs mercy against the widows, orphans and graves the ruler left behind, and the song ends in a stillness that feels less like victory than a long-held breath finally let go.
