Savage Serenade Volume 3

bfo.mantis

Savage Serenade Volume 3 keeps the series' habit of pairing calm with violence, but its calm words carry more weight: beside the gentle and quiet ones come somber, solemn, stoic and yearning, as in Somber Tempest, Solemn Edge and Stoic Rage. A few titles reverse the order, such as Savage Calm and Feral Lullaby, while Silk and Steel names a soft material and a hard one side by side, echoing the album's move from nylon strings to steel. The guitar starts out restrained, with sparse single notes and harmonics in a warm, close room, then passes through a mournful folk stretch and a fast, forceful peak before stacked guitars ring together like voices under a high vaulted roof. From there the sound thins to a last chiming harmonic and gives way to quiet, with Feral Lullaby as the closing word.

Tracklist

  1. 1
    Gentle Thunder Instrumental

    The album opens with thunder handled carefully, a power that announces itself without frightening anyone. It establishes the soft-word, strong-word pairing that runs through nearly every title that follows.

  2. 2
    Quiet Wildfire Instrumental

    A fire spreading across the hills without a sound. Early in the record, it suggests danger growing just out of earshot.

  3. 3
    Savage Calm Instrumental

    The first reversal on the album, where the wild word describes the peaceful one. Placed early, it pictures a stillness with teeth.

  4. 4
    Serene Claw Instrumental

    A predator's claw at rest, composed but ready. Closing the album's opening stretch, it keeps the threat in view while the mood stays calm.

  5. 5
    Somber Tempest Instrumental

    A storm in mourning rather than in rage, the first of the album's graver titles. It turns the record toward the sorrow of its middle section.

  6. 6
    Tranquil Blaze Instrumental

    A steady fire that burns without panic, more hearth than forest fire. In the album's middle, it offers warmth inside the melancholy.

  7. 7
    Silk and Steel Instrumental

    The only title on the album joined by 'and', naming a soft material and a hard one as equals. It also mirrors the record's overall journey from nylon-string elegance to steel-string fire.

  8. 8
    Velvet Storm Instrumental

    Rough weather wrapped in plush fabric. Near the album's center, the title keeps the storm's threat while muffling its edges.

  9. 9
    Hushed Fury Instrumental

    Rage kept to a low voice, as if someone nearby were sleeping. At the album's midpoint, the title holds its anger in check.

  10. 10
    Tender Rage Instrumental

    Anger that comes from caring too much. Past the middle of the record, the title suggests a fury rooted in love rather than hate.

  11. 11
    Yearning Howl Instrumental

    Here the howl is a cry of longing rather than threat, the sound of something calling for what it has lost. In the album's second half, it brings the lament to its rawest point.

  12. 12
    Blazing Shadow Instrumental

    Light and darkness collide in one name: a shadow that burns. As the album builds toward its peak, the title imagines a darkness that gives off heat.

  13. 13
    Silent Frenzy Instrumental

    Wild motion with no sound at all, like a storm seen through a window. Late in the record, it points to energy building fast just beneath the surface.

  14. 14
    Solemn Edge Instrumental

    A blade carried with ceremony rather than menace. Near the album's climax, the title gives sharpness a sense of ritual and weight.

  15. 15
    Serene Thunder Instrumental

    Thunder heard from a great distance, peaceful in its size. As the album enters its final stretch, the title suggests power settling into something vast and calm.

  16. 16
    Whispering Inferno Instrumental

    Embers that keep talking after the flames have died, passing along the last secrets of a fire. Near the end of the album, it treats the inferno as a memory rather than a threat.

  17. 17
    Stoic Rage Instrumental

    Rage endured without complaint, carried rather than released. As the next-to-last track, it sounds like the album's fury making peace with itself.

  18. 18
    Feral Lullaby Instrumental

    The album closes by flipping its pattern one last time: the wild word comes first, and it describes a song for sleep. It leaves an untamed creature finally soothed into stillness.
