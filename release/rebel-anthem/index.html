Rebel Anthem

bfo.mantis

Rebel Anthem is bfo.mantis at its most militant, 15 metal tracks aimed at a machine of corrupt officials, courts, central banks and surveillance. Several songs return in reworked versions, and history threads through the whole record, from the Declaration of Independence to Andrew Jackson's bank war. It swings from plain-spoken definitions of tyranny and rebellion to the most apocalyptic imagery in the catalog.
