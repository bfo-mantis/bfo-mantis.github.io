Freeborn

bfo.mantis

Freeborn is a roll-call rock anthem about the one right nobody hands you. It marches from a London pillory to an emperor's table at Worms, a Maryland farm, a Montgomery bus and a Prague square, and finds the same thing at every stop: a crown built on a million nods, brought down by a single No.

    More about this track

    Freeborn opens on a single drum and a voice in the dark, then walks the long road of one word. It passes a cart-tail in London mud, a pile of books on an emperor's table, a whip in Maryland, a bus seat in Montgomery and keys shaking in a Prague November. At every stop the crowd grows from one throat, until the last No stands bare in the silence.
