Ten tracks to start with

New to bfo.mantis? These ten tracks cover every corner of the catalog, from rock and metal to gospel, hip hop and the instrumental series. Press play on any of them for a 30-second preview.

  1. 1

    Dangerous

    Song · Rock · track 1 of DANGEROUS

    The title track of DANGEROUS sets out the idea behind much of the catalog: being peaceful is not the same as being harmless.

  2. 2

    One Man (Conviction)

    Song · Rock · track 5 of One Man INVICTUS

    The story of Desmond Doss, the unarmed medic at Okinawa, and the most tender song of courage on One Man INVICTUS.

  3. 3

    Carry the Star

    Song · Rock · single

    A gentle single about the small light everyone carries, proof that the catalog has warmth as well as thunder.

  4. 4

    Rebel Anthem

    Song · Metal · track 1 of Rebel Anthem

    The opener of the most militant album in the catalog, and the clearest statement of what it stands against.

  5. 5

    The Ridge

    Song · Hip Hop/Rap · track 2 of The Ridge

    A hook-driven retelling of the 1992 siege at Ruby Ridge that ends as a memorial to Sammy and Vicki Weaver.

  6. 6

    Crown of Ash

    Song · Folk · single

    A slow, solemn ballad sung to a fallen tyrant, weighing mercy against the widows, orphans and graves he left behind.

  7. 7

    Havilah

    Song · Christian/Gospel · track 14 of Sovereign Heart of Fire

    A gentle portrait of a woman who rebuilt her life after loss, from the worship album Sovereign Heart of Fire.

  8. 8

    Savage Surrender

    Instrumental · Folk · track 1 of Savage Serenade

    The opener of Savage Serenade, a solo acoustic guitar album where fierce and gentle are never far apart. The title says it: something wild that chooses to lay down its arms.

  9. 9

    Silent Tempest

    Instrumental · World · track 1 of Savage Serenade Volume 2

    A storm with the sound turned off opens Savage Serenade Volume 2, setting the album's pattern of a gentle word standing guard over a violent one.

  10. 10

    Tin Cathedral Reverberations

    Instrumental · Electronic · track 1 of Tinmouth Cathedral Volume 1

    The opener of Tinmouth Cathedral Volume 1, the earliest instrumental album in the catalog: a cathedral made of tin, where the sacred and the industrial share one roof.
